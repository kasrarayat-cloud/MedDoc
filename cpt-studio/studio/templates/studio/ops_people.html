People

Everyone who can sign in to a workspace, with their role and access. Turning access off in a workspace closes that workspace to the person, and ends their open sessions when it was their only one; turning it off everywhere stops sign-in. Nothing is deleted, and either can be turned on again. Each change records who at the practice asked for it.

Operators

Operator accounts open this console. They are granted and ended on the server only, never here, each on a recorded approval with a date by which it is reviewed. A grant past its review date, or with none, is flagged for the periodic access review.
