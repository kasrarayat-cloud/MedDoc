Description of procedure
After induction of general anesthesia, intravenous cefazolin was given by the anesthesia team before incision. The patient was positioned prone on a Wilson frame on a radiolucent table with all pressure points padded. The neurophysiology team obtained baseline lower extremity signals. A time-out was performed.

Anteroposterior fluoroscopy, counting from the sacrum and confirmed against the twelfth ribs, identified the T10-T11 interspace. The back and right buttock were prepped and draped. A 5 cm midline incision was made over T10-T11 and the paraspinal muscles were elevated subperiosteally from the T10 spinous process and laminae bilaterally. The caudal T10 spinous process was removed and a laminotomy of the inferior T10 lamina was made with a high-speed drill and Kerrison rongeurs. The ligamentum flavum at T10-T11 was removed in the midline to expose the dorsal epidural fat. The bony opening was limited to that needed to pass the paddle, and the dura was intact.

A paddle passer was advanced cephalad in the dorsal epidural space without resistance, followed by the 16-contact, three-column paddle electrode. Anteroposterior and lateral fluoroscopy confirmed the array in the dorsal epidural space, centered between the pedicles, from the superior endplate of T8 to the T9-T10 disc space. Physiologic midline was confirmed by stimulating through the paddle while the neurophysiology team recorded from bilateral leg muscles: responses were initially stronger on the right, the paddle was moved 2 mm to the left, and the central column then produced symmetric responses. The paddle was anchored to the interspinous ligament and fascia with two anchors and nonabsorbable suture, and a strain-relief loop was left in the subcutaneous tissue.

The tunneling tool was passed subcutaneously from the midline wound to the pocket and the electrode tail was brought through. The electrode was connected directly to the rechargeable generator without an extension or adaptor, and the set screws were tightened with the torque wrench. Repeat impedances through the generator were normal. The generator was placed in the pocket with the engraved side toward the skin, the excess lead was coiled beneath it, and the generator was sutured to the fascia.
