Description of procedure
The patient was positioned prone on a Jackson table with all pressure points padded. After prepping, draping and a time-out, a lateral fluoroscopic image localized L4-L5 and a 9 cm midline incision was made. Subperiosteal dissection exposed the L4 and L5 laminae, facets, pars and transverse processes bilaterally, preserving the L3-L4 facet capsules.

A navigation reference pin was placed in the right posterior superior iliac spine through a separate stab incision. A cone-beam CT spin was acquired and registered, and accuracy was confirmed on bony landmarks. Navigated pilot holes were drilled and tapped in the L4 and L5 pedicles bilaterally, each tract was palpated, and polyaxial titanium screws were placed.

Decompression: the L4 spinous process was removed, and the laminectomy at L4-L5 removed the caudal L4 lamina, the cephalad edge of the L5 lamina and the hypertrophied ligamentum flavum, decompressing the central canal. On the right, a partial medial facetectomy and foraminotomy freed the traversing L5 and exiting L4 roots. On the left, a complete L4-L5 facetectomy opened the transforaminal corridor and completed the left foraminal decompression, with the left L4 and L5 roots identified and protected. The central and right-sided decompression was required for the stenosis and was in addition to the left facetectomy needed for interbody access. All removed bone was cleaned, morselized and saved as local autograft.

Interbody fusion: an annulotomy was made in Kambin's triangle, a thorough discectomy was performed, and the endplates were prepared to bleeding subchondral bone. The anterior disc space was packed with local autograft mixed with allograft chips, and a porous titanium oblique cage packed with local autograft was impacted across the midline under navigation and lateral fluoroscopy.

Instrumentation: two titanium rods were contoured, seated and compressed across L4-L5, and the set screws were final-tightened.

Posterolateral fusion: the L4 and L5 transverse processes and the lateral pars were decorticated bilaterally with a burr, and the remaining graft mixture was laid along both intertransverse spaces.

A final cone-beam CT spin confirmed all four screws within the pedicles and the cage in good position. After irrigation and removal of the reference pin, a subfascial drain was placed and the wound was closed in layers. Counts were correct.
