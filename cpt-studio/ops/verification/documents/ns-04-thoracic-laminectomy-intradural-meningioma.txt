Description of procedure
General endotracheal anesthesia was induced by the anesthesia team, and total intravenous anesthesia was maintained to permit motor evoked potential monitoring. Antibiotics were given before incision. The neurophysiology technologist placed the electrodes and obtained reliable baseline somatosensory and transcranial motor evoked potentials from the upper and lower extremities.

The patient was turned prone onto a Jackson table with all pressure points padded, and the back was prepped and draped. After a time-out, anteroposterior and lateral fluoroscopy localized the level by counting from the sacrum and the twelfth rib, correlated with the preoperative whole-spine localizer; a spinal needle confirmed the T6 spinous process.

A midline incision was made from T5 to T8 and subperiosteal dissection exposed the T6 and T7 spinous processes and laminae bilaterally to the medial facets, preserving the facet capsules. The bone was soft, so en bloc removal for later replacement was judged unsafe: the T6 and T7 laminae were removed piecemeal with a rongeur, a high-speed drill and Kerrison rongeurs, together with the ligamentum flavum, and were not replaced. The inferior edge of the T5 lamina was undercut to expose the upper pole of the tumor. Both facet joints were preserved at each level. Epidural bleeding was controlled with bipolar cautery and gelatin-thrombin matrix.

The operating microscope was brought into the field and used from the dural opening through the dural closure. The dura was opened in the midline from upper T6 to lower T7 and tacked up with 4-0 sutures, and the arachnoid was opened sharply. A firm, tan, well-circumscribed tumor arose from a broad base on the left dorsolateral dura, displacing the cord ventrally and to the right, with a clear arachnoid plane; the left T7 dorsal rootlets were stretched over its lower pole. The tumor was debulked internally with the ultrasonic aspirator at low power, and the capsule was folded away from the cord and dissected free of the pia and the T7 rootlets, which were preserved. The dural base was detached with microscissors and bipolar cautery and the tumor was removed completely. A specimen was sent for frozen section, read as meningioma; the remainder went for permanent pathology. The dural attachment was coagulated thoroughly (Simpson grade II). The cord re-expanded and was pulsatile, with no residual tumor. The neurophysiology team reported stable evoked potentials throughout, with no alerts.

The dura was closed primarily with running 6-0 polypropylene. A Valsalva maneuver to 40 cm of water showed no cerebrospinal fluid leak, and a polyethylene glycol hydrogel sealant was applied over the suture line. No dural graft was needed. The fascia was closed watertight with interrupted 0 braided absorbable sutures, the subcutaneous layer with 2-0 absorbable sutures and the skin with running 3-0 nylon. No drain was placed.
