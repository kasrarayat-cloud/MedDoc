Description of procedure
The patient was positioned supine on a fracture table with the left leg in a padded well-leg holder and a padded perineal post. Under fluoroscopy, longitudinal traction and internal rotation were applied to the right leg and a closed reduction was obtained. Anteroposterior and lateral images confirmed the neck-shaft angle restored to about 130 degrees, medial cortical contact and no posterior sag. The right hip and thigh were prepped and draped in the usual sterile fashion. A time-out confirmed the patient, the procedure, the right side and the implants.

A 4 cm incision was made proximal to the tip of the greater trochanter and the fascia was divided in line with the skin. A guide pin was placed at the tip of the greater trochanter, slightly medial, and its position was confirmed on both views. The proximal femur was opened with the entry reamer. A ball-tipped guidewire was passed across the reduced fracture and down the femoral canal to the distal femoral metaphysis, centered on both views, and the nail length was measured at 380 mm. The canal was reamed in sequence to 11.5 mm. A long titanium cephalomedullary nail, 10 mm in diameter and 380 mm long with a 130-degree neck angle, was inserted by hand across the fracture without forceful impaction, and the reduction was maintained.

Through a separate lateral stab incision and the targeting arm, the guide pin for the lag screw was placed in the center of the femoral head on both the anteroposterior and lateral views, to within 5 mm of subchondral bone. The tip-apex distance was 18 mm. The path was drilled and a 95 mm lag screw was inserted under fluoroscopy. The set screw was engaged to allow controlled sliding. Traction was released and gentle compression was applied across the fracture. One distal interlocking screw, 5.0 mm by 40 mm, was placed freehand through the static distal hole by the perfect-circle technique, with bicortical purchase confirmed on fluoroscopy. The lesser trochanter fragment was not fixed separately. Final images showed a maintained reduction, the lag screw centered in the femoral head and the nail tip seated in the distal femur without anterior cortical contact.

The wounds were irrigated. The fascia was closed with 0 absorbable suture, the subcutaneous tissue with 2-0 absorbable suture and the skin with staples. Sterile dressings were applied.
