Technique
After informed consent and a time-out, a linear echoendoscope was passed through the mouth and advanced to
the second portion of the duodenum. An endosonographic survey of the esophagus, stomach and duodenum was

Findings
Pancreatic tail: a 22 x 19 mm hypoechoic, irregular solid mass abutting the splenic vein with a preserved

in the esophagus, stomach or duodenum.
Fine-needle biopsy
Under real-time endosonographic guidance, with color Doppler to avoid intervening vessels, the tail mass was
sampled from the gastric body (transgastric) with a 22-gauge fork-tip biopsy needle in 4 passes. On-site
evaluation reported adequate cellularity with atypical epithelial cells. Additional cores were placed in formalin for
histology.
Impression
1. 22 mm solid mass of the pancreatic tail, sampled by EUS-guided fine-needle biopsy (4 passes, transgastric).
2. No lymphadenopathy and no evident vascular invasion on EUS.
