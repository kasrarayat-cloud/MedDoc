History of Present Illness

59-year-old male presents for evaluation of persistent neck and shoulder pain with radiation, accompanied by low back pain, following a motor vehicle accident on 12/30/2025. Pain is constant and dull with intermittent sharp episodes. Discomfort radiates from the cervical region into the right shoulder and down the right arm.

Patient reports difficulty with cervical rotation and lifting. Has tried OTC ibuprofen 400 mg PRN with minimal relief. Sleep is disrupted by discomfort. No prior episodes of similar pain.
