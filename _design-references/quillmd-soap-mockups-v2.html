05

Audit-trail margin

desktop · 1280×800

Optimizes for forensic review — every clinical claim carries a marginal anchor pointing to its source utterance, the pipeline layer that produced it, and a confidence band. Body stays clean; the audit trail lives in the margin like academic citations. Different from v1's "Smart Highlights" which tints in-prose; this one anchors out.
