## AN UNFALSIFIABLE OBSERVATION IS NOT EVIDENCE, WHOEVER MADE IT

**Standing 2026-08-15.** A claim earns the word *measured* when something could
have made it come out the other way. If nothing could, it is a belief with a
screenshot attached.

⚠️ **REFERENCE CASE, AND IT CAME FROM THE OWNER'S SIDE.** A finding stated that
the Word export rendered a numbered list correctly while the other surfaces did
not — the fact that made it a THREE-WAY parity violation and pointed the fix at
the other two. **The screenshot's Word pane was cut off before the second list,
which is the only one that discriminates.** The observation could not have come
out false. Measured on the real signed visit afterwards, Word carried the SAME
defect: all six paragraphs on one numbering instance, no literal marker
surviving anywhere in `document.xml`.

**The cost was direction, not effort.** Believing it made Word the reference
implementation and the other surfaces the suspects — the exact inversion of the
truth, which would have "fixed" the surface that was already right.

> **Before a comparison becomes the axis of a diagnosis, ask what the
> observation would have looked like if it were false — then check that the
> evidence could show it.**

### ⚠️ THE DOUBLE TAP — TWO INVARIANT CENSUSES, ONE SLICE, THE SECOND AFTER BEING CORRECTED FOR THE FIRST

**Standing 2026-08-21. The reference case for this family, because the repeat
is the finding.**

> **BEFORE CITING A CENSUS, STATE WHAT RESULT WOULD HAVE REFUTED THE CLAIM. If
> none could, it is a COUNT, not evidence.**

Both halves of F24 Slice 1 were justified by a production measurement. Both
measurements were real, correct, and **incapable of bearing the weight put on
them** — and the second was made *after* the author had been corrected for the
first.

| # | the census | the claim it was cited for | why it could not refute |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | data rows by header/row DELTA: **+1 → 339, 0 → 331, other → 0** | *"the missing cell is the LABEL column, so widening at the front is safe"* | **delta is invariant under WHICH END is short.** A header naming its label column and dropping a trailing data column is +1 too |
| 2 | headers by whether the line above is BLANK: **20 of 45** | *"a caption one blank line up belongs to this table"* | **it counted BLANKS and never classified the line above the blank** as caption vs paragraph |

**Each licensed a fix that then wrote a measurement into the wrong place.** (1)
put a doctor's diameter into the reflux cell; (2) put a RIGHT-leg correction
into the LEFT leg — both reported as success, both refused before the change.

⚠️ **THE TELL IS GRAMMATICAL AND IT IS CHEAP TO SPOT.** Both censuses count a
**quantity** (a delta, a blank) and both claims are about a **kind** (which end,
what the line IS). *A count over the wrong axis cannot answer a question about
kind, however large and however true it is.* When the census and the claim are
not about the same property, the number is decoration.

**And note what DID find both: an adversarial pass that ran the function.** No
amount of re-reading the census would have produced either counterexample,
because the census was never wrong — it was answering a different question,
correctly, at volume.
