## ⚠️ WRITE THE UNTESTED CLAIM DOWN BEFORE THE PASS RUNS

**Standing 2026-08-22. The prevention side of the
could-not-have-come-out-otherwise family — same disease, earlier.**

> **Before running the adversarial pass, the author writes ONE sentence:**
> **"the claim I have NOT tested is ___."**

**WHY THIS, RATHER THAN "BE MORE CAREFUL".** Three consecutive slices of one arc
had a CLINICAL defect found by the pass rather than by the author's own proofs,
and each time the author had already written mutation proofs, gates and a
production census. That is not a competence signal. It is **instrument
geometry**:

> **An author's proofs are drawn from the same model of the problem that
> produced the defect, so they CANNOT surprise their owner. The pass draws from
> a different model, so it can.**

A proof written by the person holding the misunderstanding will test the claim
**as understood**, never the claim **as stated** — and all three defects lived
in exactly that gap:

| slice | claim as stated | claim as understood | what the gap cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| F5-h | *"the capture sweep is class-not-instance"* | the fields I remembered touching | `emPendingChange` read fresh, written stale |
| F24 S1 | *"the missing cell is the LABEL column"* | delta +1 means missing-at-the-front | a diameter written into the reflux cell |
| F24 S1b | *"a caption one blank up belongs to this table"* | a blank line is the only thing in the way | a RIGHT-leg correction written into the LEFT leg |

**Each was justified by a real, correct census that could not have refuted the
claim it was cited for.** Writing the untested claim down forces the author to
separate the two sentences BEFORE the pass, which is the only moment the
separation is cheap.

**HOW IT PAYS:**
- **If the sentence is honest, half the pass's work is PRE-AIMED** — hand it
  the sentence and it starts where the risk is instead of finding it.
- ⚠️ **If the sentence CANNOT BE WRITTEN, that is the finding.** An author who
  cannot name one untested claim is not describing a complete proof; they are
  describing the boundary of their own model.

**THIS ADDS NOTHING TO THE GATE AND REMOVES NOTHING FROM IT.** The pass stays
mandatory on the clinical generation and note-editing paths — three receipts say
so. This is a filter in front of it, not a substitute, and it is one sentence.

⚠️ **AND READ THE THREE RECEIPTS THE RIGHT WAY ROUND.** Every one of those
defects was caught **before a patient existed to be harmed**. That is the system
WORKING. The lesson is not that authors are unreliable — it is that
self-verification has a ceiling no amount of rigour raises, and the pass is how
this programme buys past it.
