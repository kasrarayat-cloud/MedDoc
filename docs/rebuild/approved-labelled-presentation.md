# Approved labelled narrative presentation

New jobs freeze `positioned-labelled-components-v24`. Stored v23 and earlier
jobs keep their original instructions, fact contracts, answer rendering and
validation. The provider assignment schema remains a selection of fact IDs;
provider concept names do not supply display labels.

For v24, a closed reader consumes the complete approved labelled-list meaning.
Each output label is an exact span of that approved meaning. Encounter metadata
roles come from complete current patient-owned source headers or a bounded
complete visit frame. A selected fact must match the role's exact value span,
subject, timing, polarity and complete source unit. Fact order does not determine
label order. Duplicate source roles, omitted documented components, clipped
values and unsupported requirement grammar remain visible conflicts. A failed
labelled requirement never falls back to an unlabelled supported narrative.
Source-role candidates are discovered separately from rendering permission:
unparsed metadata headers and patient-governed metadata statements retain whole
source spans as unresolved obligations. Definite foreign or other-encounter
material is excluded; unresolved ownership or dating cannot prove a blank.
Bounded punctuation-free role headers also retain obligations. Direct metadata
predicates are distinguished from future scheduled visits and references to
other people in a patient's concern; remote role words grant no association.

This finite reader recognizes the encounter summary components age and sex,
service date, treating clinician, country, setting, modality and patient status.
It does not infer missing values, rewrite source facts or change the approved
definition. Existing complete source-labelled vital measurement phrases retain
their original rendering, including units and scales, without duplicate labels.
Unrecognized labelled prose requires review instead of receiving guessed labels.

Derived presentation records exact definition/question/source hashes, literal
label spans and independently recomputed source-role associations. Generated
answer validation reruns both the source/scope gates and presentation derivation;
an altered mapping still fails if its outer digest is recomputed. The checker
receives the derived presentation and must assess each label/value association.
This proof does not replace independent checking, clinical approval or the
existing export gates. It makes no Word pagination claim.
