Doctor test guide

Open https://app.quillmd.com:9443/. Enter your existing preview access details, then sign in with your own account.

Fictional patients, notes and recordings only. This application is not approved for real patient information.

Start from a freshly loaded page. An earlier open page retains its original model and price quote for its normal validity period. Choose a form below and use its matching fictional example.

Six steps

  1. Choose a fictional patient. Use Patients to create one if needed. Its department must match the form.
  2. Choose a test form below. Inspect its questions and answer locations. Choose Use this form.
  3. Add the example notes. Select the patient and date of service, confirm synthetic notes, then Create document. The service date cannot be changed later.
  4. Save and generate once. Save any source edits first. Check the displayed maximum charge, choose Generate supported answers, then refresh until the request finishes.
  5. Review every answer and source. Correct it, accept a suggestion explicitly, or choose Leave blank — reviewed where offered. Save answers and review decisions.
  6. Finalize and download. Confirm your review, choose Finalize document, then Export original format ↓. Check the resulting Word document.

Use each question's exact label and a complete answer on its own line. Missing details stay missing. Assessment and plan come from your documented clinical decisions.

SOAP test — fictional ankle sprain

Navigation: Workspace → Form library → Owner demo — SOAP (typed or voice) → Use this form → mapping version 1. This custom Word form has four questions: Subjective, Objective, Assessment and Plan. The current application has no built-in Use SOAP note button.

Choose Synthetic Alex Morgan — owner demo in the X-ray department. Paste these notes:

Subjective: Synthetic Alex Morgan reports left ankle pain after stepping off a curb yesterday. No head injury.
Objective: Mild swelling over the left lateral ankle. No deformity. Distal sensation intact.
Assessment: Left ankle sprain.
Plan: Rest, ice, compression, elevation, and follow-up in one week.

What to check: All four answers belong in their matching SOAP section. Preserve both negative findings and the supplied assessment and plan. Review every source before saving and exporting.

Voice test

Create a new document with empty notes, then choose Use voice on its document page. Record the matching fictional example or upload a recording. Listen, confirm consent, check the charge, then choose Transcribe recording.

  1. Record up to five minutes, or upload one audio track in .wav, .webm, .mp3 or .m4a, up to 15 MB.
  2. Send it within 30 minutes of opening the page and before preview access ends.
  3. Review the returned transcript. Correct punctuation, names, numbers and units; rewrite it as the selected form's labelled answers.
  4. Choose Append or Replace, confirm your review, then Save reviewed transcript to notes. Generate and review as above.

Limits: Speech recognition can be wrong. A raw transcript never replaces your notes without your review. Synthesized-speech engineering tests do not establish human dictation accuracy. On the separate newer preview, transcription is currently refused because usage rates are not configured.

Refused: old .doc files; encrypted, password-protected or scanned forms; other Word content controls, text boxes, tracked changes, fields or macros; web/email links or linked images; PDFs with choices, signing fields, buttons, links, attachments or actions. The upload page names the reason.

Signing: A signature is never generated from notes. Where offered, type your exact account name or leave it empty. Every prewritten WM IOV statement needs separate review. CPT remains unavailable without its required licence.

Headings, corrections and underlined-Tab sentences can hold answers for review
  • A heading or forward pointer can make later answers need your decision. A final heading or backward pointer can affect earlier answers. Corrections can also hold earlier answers.
  • Use exact labels for question/answer tables, placeholders, fillable PDFs and WM IOV. In a note with several nonempty lines, an unlabelled line or a label without an answer can hold filled answers for your decision. Missing answers stay blank.
  • Underlined-Tab forms can use complete sentences with a recognised verb and final punctuation. Headings, quotations, incomplete lines and words such as if, these or above can hold answers.
  • Read the full notes and the reason beside each held answer. Make an explicit decision; do not assume an empty answer means a negative finding.

Save regularly. Account and preview sessions end after 30 minutes without activity; preview access also ends two hours after sign-in. Review every generated answer and compare the downloaded document with your source notes.
