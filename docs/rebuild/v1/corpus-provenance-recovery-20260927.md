## Authorized metadata protocol before exact selection

1. A custodian with fresh context receives this protocol and source metadata,
   not parser/model outputs. Never read the historical failure results to choose
   easier forms. Preserve the original 80 records, seals and all first passes.
2. Verify exact-file blankness, source URL/revision/hash, license/notice scope,
   embedded-asset rights, intended clinical use and underlying template lineage.
   Record supporting evidence and uncertainty. Rejecting ineligible rights is
   recorded as an eligibility decision with reason, never a compatibility pass.
   Files already frozen remain in their original historical denominator.
3. Predeclare the **new qualification** as 20 independent lineages, one file each,
   source-disjoint from every development/historical-holdout lineage and source
   group. Preserve all original Q20 records even where only one member of a pair
   enters the separate new cohort. Retain at least three source groups and a
   maximum of eight files per group; retain at least one original legacy DOC.
4. Retain the original structural constraint: each F1–F10 has at least two
   independent qualification cases; the overall compatibility evidence must
   retain at least four independent examples per family and ten sources. Score
   every declared family at 75% or higher with positive examples, regardless of
   aggregate success. Do not rename families to hide absent support.
5. Check metadata feasibility before choosing exact files. The twelve reserved
   lineages have 256 one-per-lineage combinations; **192 meet at least two cases
   in every family using recorded tags alone**. No combination was selected or
   exposed. F1 and F7 each have conservative minimum 1 / possible maximum 4 across
   those choices, so arbitrary first-file selection is unsafe. Other minimums:
   F2 6, F3 5, F4 6, F5 11, F6 9, F8 2, F9 4, F10 5. These are collector tags,
   not a parser success or independent annotation result.
6. Before reading candidate outputs, freeze a deterministic selection rule over
   eligible metadata, the source quotas, all exact hashes and every structural
   denominator. Use a fixed recorded seed or hash ordering with the declared
   coverage constraints; do not search seeds after seeing compatibility. If the
   constraints are infeasible, record the precise missing structural/rights
   requirement and acquire only that evidence before freezing.
7. Independently annotate expected answer areas from originals/renderings before
   candidate output. Seal annotations from developers. Freeze the reviewed
   software/model/profile before the one final acceptance attempt. Keep all
   first-pass results, refusals and assisted outcomes; require 18/20 automatic
   successes and no silent omission/wrong destination/meaning corruption.
8. Use missing, ordinary and long answers, complete app workflows, paired renders
   and authorized native Word checks. Existing unchanged valid evidence may be
   reused with explicit identity. No live paid campaign is authorized by this
   metadata protocol. Existing financial/account/model gates remain in force.
