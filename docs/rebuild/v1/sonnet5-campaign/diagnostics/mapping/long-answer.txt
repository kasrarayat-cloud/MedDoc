Medicines on discharge: the oral antibiotic until the course is complete, the usual inhaler twice daily with the spacer, paracetamol when needed for discomfort, and the reduced dose of the blood pressure tablet. The inhaler technique was checked by the ward pharmacist before discharge and a written list of every change was given to the patient and to the carer.
Investigations: repeat radiograph on day ten showed partial clearing of the consolidation. Sputum culture grew a sensitive organism and the antibiotic was changed to an oral agent on day seven. Treatment and progress: mobility returned to the level before admission with a frame, and the occupational therapist confirmed safe transfers at home. Plan after discharge: complete the oral course, attend the respiratory clinic in six weeks with a repeat radiograph, and return urgently if breathlessness or fever comes back. The family doctor is asked to review the blood pressure medicines, which were reduced during the admission.
