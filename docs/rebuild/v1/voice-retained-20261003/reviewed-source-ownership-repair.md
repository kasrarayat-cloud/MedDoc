The new helper requires an exact immutable, explicitly confirmed reviewed
transcript span. It proves a closed nominal phrase directly after one exact
unique own-field heading, or a completed passive/expletive observation whose
complete grammatical theme matches the original question. It uses grammatical
structure rather than a larger clinical noun list. It rejects personal
pronouns, named people, foreign ownership, quoted speech, modal/hypothetical
facts, past or bounded time, cessation and unresolved source scope. An exact
bare heading selected as a qualifier is retained with its proved adjacent body.
Missing qualifier IDs still refuse; the application never repairs the wire.

The old question-reference index coupled the entire unlabelled paragraph.
The new proof checks the actual references and allows only the proved source,
its exact own heading and complete numeric metadata using that field name as a
modifier of another nominal head (`Procedure date, 09/21/2026`). Clinical
modifiers, cancellations, uncertainty, future metadata and additional
unselected assertions retain their holds.

The actual whole 005 response selects `No polyps were seen.` for Findings and
Polyps. Even after its signed ordinary relation proved the Polyps destination,
the legacy one-statement/two-specific-questions guard held it. The exemption
now requires that unit's bounded reviewed-source proof for that destination.
A merely valid ordinary relation does not receive that exemption. Repeated
questions, numbered competitors and the old two-topic clinical statement remain
held by their existing guards.

A new counterexample initially released `No polyps were seen.` beside
`The examination was entered in error.`. The first unsafe output is preserved.
The bounded proof now refuses when an abstract clinical annotation is explicitly
entered in error. A bounded numeric pain-score correction is a distinct source
