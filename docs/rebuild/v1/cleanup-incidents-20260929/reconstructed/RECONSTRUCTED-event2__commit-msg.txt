Refutation repairs RS-1..RS-5 and RA-LOW-1: an in-doubt settlement asserts nothing; one rule for account emails

A refutation of the residuals assessment (docs/rebuild/v1/residuals-assessment-20260929.md) on 61c5cc8f found:

RS-1 (MEDIUM). settle()'s COMMIT sent and its answer lost: the handler reconnected, read Usage.exists()
before the in-flight commit was visible and recorded 'reconciliation' with "... nothing is charged until
it is resolved" while the charge committed a moment later. Reproduced deterministically (the COMMIT's
answer lost at the Django connection layer; the transaction stays open on its server connection holding
its locks and is committed or rolled back only after the handler waits for one of them): the job said
nothing was charged while a -0.15 debit, the usage row and the settled holds of that commit existed.
core.jobs.commit_in_doubt(error, carrier) is structural, like never_transmitted: the exception passed
through Django's own commit() and the connection that carried the COMMIT is gone (Django reconnects at
once inside atomic's exit, so identity is the signal, not absence). An in-doubt settlement is held:
'reconciliation', "The settlement outcome of this request is unknown; an administrator must reconcile
it.", nothing released, absorbed or freed. A COMMIT the server refused (deferred FK) is still an
established rollback. The handler now writes under the job's row lock and never overwrites an outcome a
committed transaction recorded: the adoption's COMMIT answer lost after it committed used to turn a
delivered, charged success into a platform failure with the charge reversed. admin_resolve_job already
resolves from the committed rows under the job lock; tests prove both ways (landed: charge reversed once,
no hold released; not landed: holds released, receipt cost absorbed).

RS-2 (LOW). The handler first does Django's request-boundary connection repair (a connection broken
outside a transaction stayed broken: OperationalError out of run_one, job left 'running'); a handler
read or write that fails holds the job with "The outcome of this request could not be recorded; an
administrator must reconcile it." on a fresh connection. An unreachable database still leaves it to the
stale sweep.

RS-3 (LOW, money). The failed state and the absorption are one transaction; a failed absorption leaves
nothing of it and holds the job with its key and charge (usage.absorb_failed audited) for the
administrator's resolution, never 'failed', charged and re-requestable. core.mapping_jobs had the same
shape and is repaired the same way. test_billing_policy pinned the old behaviour and is updated.

(OperationalError) ..."); the key is freed only when no charge can exist (an in-doubt settlement holds).
