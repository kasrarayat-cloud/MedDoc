**Voice-first.** The 14-unit ankle transcript that the provider refused under v2 compiled and answered under v3 (5,320 in /
2,657 out, `end_turn`): the schema repair is confirmed live. The model selected the right units and stated every
neighbour independent, but the app held all five answers by its neighbour rule, because smart formatting makes a spoken

dictation in one request, and a fail-closed release for a heading that is exactly the asked field's own label, are being
refuted and will be re-measured.

**Correction (2026-10-02): the colonoscopy stop was the application's rate limit, not a batch.** The first version of
this section said v3's larger answers split the colonoscopy transcript into a multi-part batch. That was a guess from
the runner's stop message, and the database disproves it. The campaign's QA database holds exactly **30** generation jobs
between `02:34:40Z` and `02:41:44Z` (model check 1, notes 28, ankle 1) and no 31st. `core.views.generate_view` admits
**30 Generate requests per user per 600 s** (`core.security.rate_limit`), so the colonoscopy Generate was refused
(`Generation rate limit reached.`) and redirected without a job. The runner saw "not exactly one job" and stopped with
nothing sent. Its Deepgram transcription (attempt 32) completed and is costed; the transcript was never generated. The
runner now (`fix/v2-standing-generate-pacing`) reads the application's own window and waits it out before a send that
leads to a Generate — for a recording, before the recording is sent — honouring the Stop file every second, and names
the page's message when a Generate is refused.

("Symptoms."), phrases its findings without a subject or as "The clinician explicitly documents ...", and names both
ankles (a right-ankle denial). Whether a dictated note that never presents the patient may have its subjectless
statements read as the patient's is an owner decision (it widens attribution on the clinical path), not a defect
to repair here. The colonoscopy recording was dictated onto an intake form it does not fit; most of its fields have
nothing to answer, as expected.
