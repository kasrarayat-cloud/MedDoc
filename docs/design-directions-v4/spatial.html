Manifesto
The room is the product.

This is SPATIAL. We believe medical software should be a place, not a screen — a quiet, dimly-lit room where a patient's chart drifts toward you and the rest of the practice recedes into focus-pull blur. We refuse flat layouts, gridded cards, and 2D chrome. Every visit lives at a real depth in a real scene; the recording is not a button-with-a-waveform but a luminous sphere that breathes with the doctor's voice; the dashboard is not a table but a constellation of cards you walk through. We commit to WebGL as the substrate, parallax as the language of attention, and depth-of-field as the tool that says this patient, right now, in front of you. Linear, Vercel, Stripe — they built screens. We are building a room.
