This is Minimalist. We believe medical software should disappear. We refuse hierarchy that demands attention, decoration that adds nothing, and the false productivity of dense chrome. The page is mostly empty on purpose. The accent is a single muted rust hairline that asks for nothing. Patient names are large because they matter. Buttons are text. Status is a word. Motion is a fade. The magic is the absence of magic.

62-year-old woman with known HFrEF (LVEF 35% on 2025-11 echo), hypertension, and CKD stage 3 returns for follow-up two weeks after dose increase of metoprolol succinate to 50 mg daily. She reports resolution of morning lightheadedness but new exertional dyspnea on climbing two flights of stairs, with subjective bilateral lower-extremity edema noted by her husband. Denies chest pain, pressure, palpitations, orthopnea, or PND. No medication changes since last visit. Adherent to fluid restriction (1.5 L/day) and low-sodium diet.

o

BP 132/84, HR 68 reg, RR 16, SpO2 97% on room air, weight 71.2 kg (up 1.4 kg from prior visit).

General — well-appearing, in no acute distress. CV — regular rate and rhythm, soft S3 at apex, no murmurs. Pulm — bibasilar fine crackles, clearing with cough. Ext — trace bilateral pitting edema to mid-shin. JVP estimated at 8 cm.

a

1. HFrEF, mildly decompensated. Volume overload signs (weight gain, S3, crackles, edema) without acute distress. Likely related to dietary indiscretion vs. progression; consider diuretic up-titration.

2. HTN, controlled. BP at goal on current regimen.

3. CKD st. 3. Stable; last eGFR 47 (2026-03-08).

p

— Increase furosemide from 20 mg to 40 mg PO daily × 5 days, then reassess. Continue metoprolol succinate 50 mg daily and lisinopril 10 mg daily.

— Daily morning weights; call office if gain > 1 kg in 48 h or new orthopnea.

— Basic metabolic panel in 5–7 days to monitor potassium and creatinine.

— Reinforce 1.5 L fluid restriction and 2 g sodium diet.

— Follow-up in 2 weeks; sooner if symptoms worsen.
