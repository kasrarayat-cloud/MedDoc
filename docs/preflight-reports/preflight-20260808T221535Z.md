### Note — Caddyfile inode divergence (not a fault)

`check-bind-mount-agreement` reports the Caddyfile's **content agrees** but its
**inode has diverged** (655521 ≠ 655733). Per AGENTS.md this is the expected
consequence of a branch switch or a write-then-rename edit, and it re-arms
itself. It is a note, not a RED.

The operational consequence, carried forward so it is not rediscovered
mid-incident: **the next edit to `Caddyfile` will NOT reach the container.**
Use `--force-recreate`, never `caddy reload` (which will report "config is
unchanged") and never `caddy validate` (which will validate the OLD file).
