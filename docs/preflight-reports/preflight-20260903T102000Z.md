It was still declined, for two reasons:

1. **Disk is not the constraint.** 76% used with 71G free. The trigger exists to
   protect headroom, and headroom is fine. Ruling #137's whole point is that the

   Nothing forces the two to be adjacent, so they should not be.

decision and needs its reasoning written down.
