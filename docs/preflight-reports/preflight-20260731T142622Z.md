## The image-reclaim trigger, and why it is being declined AGAIN — with a correction

Check 2's trigger (images reclaimable > 5 GB) is met at **127.9 GB**. It is declined, for two reasons, the second of which corrects what the 2026-07-31T07:10Z report implied.

1. **`docker image prune -a` removes unused TAGGED images**, which includes the
   **11 `pre-*` rollback tags** this project's runbook (§3.1–3.2) treats as
   load-bearing. Six of them are already older than a 168h window.

2. **The growth driver is a CO-TENANT, not this project.** The earlier report left
   the impression that meddoc builds were filling the disk. They are not:
   `voiceassistmain-agent:git-*` carries **~10 images of ~1.5 GB each built in the
   last 17 hours**, plus `openclaw:local` at 4.26 GB. The preflight skill puts
   non-meddoc containers explicitly out of scope, and pruning another tenant's
   images would reach outside this project's boundary and could destroy their own
   rollback path. **Not ours to reclaim.**

Applied instead: `docker image prune -f` (dangling only, 9 untagged layers — unreferenced by any container and owned by nobody). Disk before → after: 71G → 71G.

**If disk ever crosses 80%,** the correct action is a tag-aware sweep of THIS project's images (keep the N most recent `pre-*` tags, prune the rest by digest) plus a conversation with the co-tenant owner — never a blanket `-a`.
