## Deliberate deviation from the skill's Check-2 auto-fix

Check 2 nominally triggers `docker image prune -af --filter "until=168h"` when
images-reclaimable exceeds 5 GB (it is 138.7 GB). **That auto-fix was not run,
for two reasons, and this is a considered deviation rather than an omission:**

1. **It would delete the rollback tags this very task depends on.** `-a` removes
   every image not referenced by a *running* container, which is precisely what
   a rollback tag is. The R2 brief requires "deploy via `scripts/deploy-prod.sh`
   with rollback tags first". `meddoc-pro-transcribe-md-worker:pre-verifierfix-20260727`
   sits at exactly 7 days — on the `until=168h` boundary — so the filter is not
   a reliable guard here.
2. **The bulk of the reclaimable space is another project's.** The image census
   is 74 × `voiceassistmain-agent` against 12 + 12 + 3 for the meddoc repos.
   Per this skill's own scope rule, non-meddoc containers/images are out of
   scope and are not mine to delete unattended.

Disk at 81% with **56 G available** is comfortably above what the R2 build chain
needs (~10–20 G for `next build` + a separately-tagged QA image). No reclaim was
necessary to proceed. If disk crosses 90%, the correct first move is a
**scoped** reclaim of `voiceassistmain-agent` (74 images) with that project's
owner, not a blanket `prune -af`.
