## Deliberate deviation from the Check-2 auto-fix

**`docker image prune -af --filter "until=168h"` was NOT run**, and this is a
ruling, not an omission.

- The trigger ("Images reclaimable > 5 GB") is met at 45.24 GB, but the
  *reason* for the trigger is not: disk is at **50% used with 144 G free**,
  far below the 80% yellow line. There is nothing to relieve.
- A blanket `-af` prune conflicts with two standing rulings. The owner's disk-
  reclaim contract is explicit — *"the table is the contract; nothing it names
  gets pruned"* — and it names every co-tenant image (`voiceassistmain`,
  `leathererp`, `dealmason`, `dealhunter`) and **the current rollback set**.
  `-af` respects neither.
- The rollback tags are the operative risk today: this session is about to cut
  new ones for a worker deploy, and destroying the old ones to reclaim space
  that is not needed would be trading a safety net for nothing.

Recorded here rather than silently skipped, so the next preflight does not read
a green Check 2 as "there was nothing to prune".
