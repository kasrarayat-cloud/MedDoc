### 🟡 The Layer-4 deviation: drawer inner-section slate-* preservation

UserDetailDrawer.client.tsx (665 LOC) and InvestigateDrawer.client.tsx (289 LOC) have:

- **Outer shell + header + footer**: fully Clinician (cream-card, hairline border, paper shadow, sage/amber/coral accent dots, qm-cl-btn-* footer buttons)
- **Inner sections** (ActionGroup/ActionChip/DrawerSection/DefList/ProviderBar in UserDetail; Section/Field helpers in Investigate): kept slate-* Tailwind utility classes (48 slate-* refs in UserDetailDrawer; 19 in InvestigateDrawer)

Subagent's reasoning (verbatim from their summary): "The drawer's inner sections … keep their slate-* utility classes; they render acceptably against the warm cream backdrop because the parent shell switches palette and the slate gradients are subtle. This was a deliberate budget call — the file is 665 LOC and a full parallel tree would have ~doubled it without matching visual ROI."

**Pro:**
- Risk-bounded: outer shell flips, inner content readable
- Budget-respectful: doesn't spend 700 LOC of duplication for incremental visual ROI
- Reversible: a follow-up commit can port the inner sections cleanly later

**Caveat:**
- Visual inconsistency: the slate-* gradients on a cream backdrop ARE legible but mismatched
- The other 8 admin client files DID get fully parallel ClinicianXxx implementations — these two are outliers

**User call:** accept the budget tradeoff (recommended unless visual mismatch is unacceptable on a real browser pass) OR ask for a follow-up to port the inner sections.
