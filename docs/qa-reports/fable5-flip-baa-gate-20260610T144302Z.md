## Blocker 1 — Fable 5 cannot run under Zero Data Retention; it mandates 30-day PHI retention (positively confirmed)

Fable 5 is a **Mythos-class ("Covered") model**. Per Anthropic's own Privacy Center:

- *"Prompts submitted to, and outputs generated by, Mythos-class models are retained for **30 days** for trust and safety purposes, **on every platform where these models are offered.**"* — [Data retention practices for Mythos-class models](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/15425996-data-retention-practices-for-mythos-class-models)
- *"Claude Fable 5 shares the same underlying model as Claude Mythos 5"* (same retention class).
- **ZDR is NOT available** for covered models: ZDR workspaces must **turn retention ON** to access these models. Per the BAA article, *"for Covered Models, we require limited data retention and review as a part of our safety work."* — [BAA for Commercial Customers](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/8114513-business-associate-agreements-baa-for-commercial-customers)
- Web-search corroboration: *"Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 are designated Covered Models and require 30-day data retention. Zero data retention is not available for Claude Fable 5 or Claude Mythos 5."*

**Why this is a hard blocker for QuillMD specifically.** The generation layers (L3–L6, Rx drafting, preview action) process **PHI** — cleaned transcript text, SOAP notes, Rx drafts, referral letters, patient summaries (per `docs/BAA_CHECKLIST.md` §"What each processor sees"). Our entire HIPAA posture is built on **requesting zero-retention** from Anthropic:

- `docs/BAA_CHECKLIST.md:149` — *"Request zero-retention via the Anthropic data retention policy."*
- `docs/HIPAA_COMPLIANCE.md` treats sub-processor PHI retention as a tracked risk surface.

Flipping generation to Fable 5 would silently create a **new 30-day PHI-retention footprint at a sub-processor** that the BAA scope, the COMPLIANCE_REGISTER risk assessment, and the data-flow diagram do **not** account for. That is a material change to PHI handling requiring operator risk-acceptance + a compliance-register update + (if pursued) a BAA that explicitly enables retention for the covered-model workspace — **not** an autonomous engineering flip. The parse/PHI-scan calls staying on Opus does not help: the generation layers alone carry the full PHI payload.
