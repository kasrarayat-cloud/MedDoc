other. Both preparation prompts establish the intended party from complete form
context, preserve distinct clinician/guardian/witness targets and leave true
ambiguity unresolved. Guided review and definition editors explain the missing
subject. Both retained automatic responses remain untouched. Historical sealed
definitions, source/answer proofs and exports use the unchanged readers.
