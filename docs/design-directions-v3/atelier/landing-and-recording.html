The chart is not the visit. The chart is what remains of the visit, written down, after the room has emptied and the patient has gone home and you, the physician, are alone with a keyboard. Quill&MD listens to the visit while you conduct it, and writes the chart for you in the room's own language — the patient's words, your assessment, your plan — rendered in the format your service requires, ready for your review and your signature, and nothing more.

We do not animate the AI for you. We do not show you orbs. We have decided that a doctor scanning a chart at 7:45 PM on a Tuesday does not require theater. We will, instead, show you the words. Whether they are correct is the only question that matters, and the only question we have built this product to help you answer.
