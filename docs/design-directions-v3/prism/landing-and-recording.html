What you'll see

A live thought-chain, not a spinner.

QuillMD streams every reasoning step the model takes — "weighing dyspnea on exertion vs ambiguous chest pressure" — so you never wonder whether something is happening.

What you'll never see

A surprise diagnosis or a hallucinated drug.

Layer 5 cross-checks every prescription against the patient's allergy chart and the day's spoken dosage — and stops the workflow if anything mismatches.

What's audited

Every layer. Every prompt. Every cent.

Each layer logs prompt hash, token count, cost in cents, and the diff it produced. Your compliance officer reviews it; the AI doesn't get a private channel.
