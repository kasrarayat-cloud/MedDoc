Designer’s note The idea in one line: money is a record before it is a dashboard, so the screen is a ruled book you read in one sequence and the act of setting a rate has the weight of signing a page. I banned myself from cards, tiles, gauges, charts, sparklines, neon-glow status panels and any side-by-side before/after — the history is a list of entries, never a diff. G

Notes on the tokens — what I broke, and who has to decide

Broken — the leaf is paper
The whole record surface uses --rounds-paper-1/2/3, --rounds-ink, --rounds-ink-muted and --rounds-paper-line-1/2. Those are shipped tokens, but today they dress only the clinical document; this would be the first admin surface to use them. The argument is that the money record is a document too, and it inherits the same reading posture as a note. The decision to carry: does “paper” mean clinical in this product, or does it mean record? If it means clinical, this direction needs a fifth surface token and the paper palette stays where it is.
Broken — radii to zero
--rounds-radius-sm/md/lg (9 / 12 / 18 px) are used nowhere on the leaf. A page has square corners; a bound book has no rounded rectangles in it, and the moment a rounded container appears the whole thing reads as a card again. Cheap to reverse, and reversing it costs the direction most of its identity.
Broken — serif as UI type
--rounds-font-clinical is the document serif; the shell’s UI font is a sans. Here the serif is the interface face too, with --rounds-font-mono carrying every figure, id, timestamp and label. No webfont is loaded — this file is standalone, so it falls back through Iowan Old Style / Palatino / Georgia. Real cost: the rendering will differ between machines until a face is chosen and self-hosted.
Kept — accent as text
The token note says #5b5bd6 fails AA as small text on the stage, which is why --rounds-accent-text #a5a5f0 exists. On paper the base accent measures about 5.3:1, so it is used directly for the IN EFFECT mark, the stamped row ids and the links — and #a5a5f0 is used only on the dark stage captions. Same two tokens, each in the ground it was made for.
Kept — status vocabulary
HEALTHY · DEGRADED · DOWN, unchanged, no fourth word. On paper they take the on-light variants #187a4e, #c4841c, #c93a2e; the -text variants stay for the dark stage. The only addition is a clause of cause under the word, because “charge metering — DOWN” is alarming and correct, and DOWN by design, not by fault is the sentence that stops somebody paging themselves at 02:00.
Invented — six light neutrals
No token covers the edge of a block of paper, so the fore-edge and binding use #c9cbc2 / #a9aca2, and four tints carry field and notice states: #f6f6fd (focus), #fdf4f3 (refused), #fdf8ee and #7a5310 (read-only). All six want either real tokens or deletion before this ships — they are the least defensible thing on the page.
Unresolved
--rounds-paper-shadow arrived truncated in the token extract, so the leaf’s drop shadow here is invented (0 26px 54px rgba(0,0,0,.5)). Swap it for the real value before anyone judges the depth.
Not designed — on purpose
The lock. Everything under /admin already inherits the SUPER_ADMIN re-read, notFound(), the 12 h freshness check and the access audit row, so the only place the gate appears in this direction is where it is visible to the operator: the 404 leaf, and the freshness clause in every foot rule.
