`claude-sonnet-5-5`; `inference_geo: us` metered while unset; output at or above 70% of the request's output limit; a
validator, export or billing-audit failure; any unsafe qualified typed output (an answer adopted without source support,

unscannable evidence tree. A missing, malformed or internally inconsistent qualified classification refuses further
sends. The stricter qualified-stage stop is kept alongside the original worker verdict; it never rewrites old veto
evidence. For transcription
also: a request other than the declared one, or not the submission the product prepared; a receipt naming another
model; a processed duration whose provider charge at the declared rate exceeds the send's maximum, or a settlement
that differs from it; an empty transcript, a disclosed incomplete or unknown coverage, or a stored transcript that is
not the provider's; a reviewed transcript whose exact prospective raw source does not match, whose explicit edits differ from the declared freeze, or whose saved notes/audit/review proof differ from the declared review. A safety failure stops
the affected stage permanently in this campaign. A repaired candidate is measured under a fresh distinct campaign
identity after an offline diagnosis, committed repair and renewed qualification; the failed evidence is retained and
every original verdict stays as recorded.
