### The clinical row — as ATTRIBUTABLE MOVEMENT, not as a frozen number

⚠️ **This is a LIVE system and a doctor may sign a note during the deploy.** The
old form of this row ("counts must be identical") produces a false finding the
first time real work happens mid-deploy, and a row that cries wolf gets waived.

**The standing form: every count either holds, or MOVES BY AN AMOUNT AN AUDIT ROW
EXPLAINS.**

- `transcribe_visits` 58, SIGNED 39, `transcribe_md_patients` 46 — any delta must
  be matched by `audit_logs` rows in the deploy window naming the actor and act.
- `users` 198 / `practices` 199 — no signup path is expected to fire; a delta here
  is a finding until an audit row explains it.
- `template_twin_certifications` 3 / `fill_manifests` 5 — **unchanged is expected**;
  this deploy touches no twin code.
- `audit_logs` 12361 — expected to GROW (deploy activity itself writes rows). Its
  growth is the *instrument* for the rows above, not a number to freeze.

⚠️ **A difference that is not in this declaration IS THE FINDING**, whatever its
explanation sounds like after the fact.
