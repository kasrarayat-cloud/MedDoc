### ⚠️ Rows 7 and 19 are this deploy's own assertions, and they are the point

Every other row is the standard clinical-count set, carried because a deploy
that moves a visit, patient or signature count has done something nobody asked
for. These two exist because of what this specific change could do if it were
wrong:

- **Row 19 is checked INSIDE the running containers, never on the compose
  file.** `docker-compose.qa.yml`'s `${MOCK_AI_CALLS:-true}` is the recorded
  precedent for why: the file read as safe and the containers were not, because
  a `${VAR:-default}` inherits from the shared `.env` that production needs. If
  `BILLING_ENFORCED` ever reaches a container by that route, the compose file
  will not say so.
- **Row 7 is what row 19 failing would LOOK like.** If the flag were somehow on,
  every practice has a spendable balance of 0, admission refuses every visit,
  and `FAILED` climbs off zero. Two independent statements of one invariant —
  one structural, one behavioural — because the structural check is the one that
  can be true about the wrong universe.

### On rows 9 and 10

`api_usage_events` and `ai_calls` are live counters: a visit processed during
the deploy window moves them legitimately. They are still declared exactly.
**A difference that is not in this declaration is THE FINDING** — and if either
moves, the correct response is to identify the visit that moved it, not to reach
for *"the deploy window explains that"*.
