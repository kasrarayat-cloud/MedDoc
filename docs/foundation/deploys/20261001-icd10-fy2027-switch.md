## ⚠️ WHY THIS ONE GETS THE STRICT FORM

A code-set swap is a **billing-surface** change that alters what the product
will accept from a doctor, on a date set by an external authority. Fifteen codes
that are billable on 2026-09-30 are refused on 2026-10-01. A rollback restores
the old image, but any note signed in between was coded under whichever edition
was live, and that is not undone by redeploying.

## ⚠️ A SECOND VISIBLE-CHANGE CLASS, AND IT IS HARSHER THAN THE FLIPS

**15 codes billable in FY2026 are GONE from FY2027 entirely** — not reclassified,
deleted:

`S23.420A/D/S` · `T52.8X1A/D/S` · `T52.8X2A/D/S` · `T52.8X3A/D/S` · `T52.8X4A/D/S`

⚠️ **These refusals CANNOT name a replacement.** A flip refuses and offers
children; a deletion refuses and offers nothing, because CMS removed the concept.
A doctor dictating `T52.8X1A` on 2026-10-01 gets a bare refusal, and that is
correct behaviour rather than a defect in the token.

⚠️ **AND THE TWO FIFTEENS ARE A COINCIDENCE.** 15 flips and 15 deletions are
different sets with nothing in common; do not let a matching count invite the
inference that they are the same event seen twice.

## ⚠️ THE ROW THAT WILL LOOK LIKE A REGRESSION AND IS NOT

A doctor who dictated `I42.0` on 2026-09-30 and gets a refusal on 2026-10-01 has
met **CMS's change, not a defect**. The refusal must name `I42.00 / I42.01 /
I42.09`. If it refuses WITHOUT naming children, that IS a defect — measured
2026-09-05, all 15 flips have billable children, so an empty child list cannot
be correct.

## The release note says it plainly

> **ICD-10-CM updated to FY2027, effective 1 October 2026.** 238 codes are newly
> accepted. Fifteen codes that were billable are now category headers — including
> dilated cardiomyopathy (`I42.0`), plantar fascial fibromatosis (`M72.2`) and
> BMI (`Z68.1`) — and coding now asks for the specific child code instead,
> naming it for you. This follows CMS's annual release; it is not a change of
> our own.
