## ⚠️ THE ONE ROW WHOSE WORDING WAS WRONG — stated, not smoothed over

§4 declared `audit_logs` as *"**10619**, or higher only by rows a **human**
caused during the window."*

**The count moved 10619 → 10626 before the deploy, and no human caused it.** The
seven rows landed at **02:00:15–02:00:32Z** from two synthetic accounts,
`qa-tmd@quillmd.local` and `qa-bundle@quillmd.local`: three `LOGIN_SUCCESS`,
three `VIEWED_VISIT_LIST`, one `LOGIN_FAILED`. All read-only; no visit, patient
or signature was touched, which the unmoved clinical counts independently
confirm.

**The substance of the row held and is the part that matters:** the
immediately-pre-deploy re-measure was **10626** and the post-deploy measure is
**10626**, so *the deploy itself wrote zero audit rows* — which is what the row
existed to check. What failed was my predicate: I wrote "a human" where the
honest declaration was **"activity outside this deploy"**.

⚠️ **AND THE SOURCE IS NOT ESTABLISHED — recorded as open rather than guessed.**
The obvious candidate is `nightly-tests.yml`, whose cron is `0 2 * * *` and which
targets `https://app.quillmd.com`. But **`gh run list` shows no nightly run for
2026-08-10 at all**, and no GitHub workflow ran in that window — the nearest were
02:01:50Z (this merge commit, on `main`) and 02:10:12Z (PR #574). So something on
the **host** authenticated to production on a schedule. I could not enumerate
host cron: `crontab -l` is **not granted** to the loop and is **not in MASTER.md's
known-constraints table**, and the sandbox refuses `ls /etc/cron.d` (working
directory is restricted to `/opt/meddoc-pro`). Both refusals are reported in the
session log as findings.

**The `LOGIN_FAILED` is the part worth a person's eye** — a scheduled process
that authenticates to production and fails one of its logins is either a broken
smoke or something nobody is watching.
