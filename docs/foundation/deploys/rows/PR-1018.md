## ⚠️ WHAT THESE ROWS DO NOT CLAIM

- **Not that a doctor can complete the flow.** These are structural presence
  checks on the image plus read-only counts. The clinical walk belongs on QA,
  against a build proven byte-identical to what is promoted.
- **Not that `omit` fires.** The resolver additionally requires
  `conceptUnmatched && reason === undefined && facts.length > 0` — all three are
  properties of a particular encounter. The gates prove the choice CAN matter;
  nothing here proves it WILL on any given visit.
- **N3 is asserted at the RESOLUTION level, not the byte level.** That the DOCX
  bytes then match is `gate-157b-denial-contract`'s property, proved there and
  not re-proved here.
