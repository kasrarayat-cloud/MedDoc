### ⚠️ ROW 4 IS A REAL PREDICTION THIS TIME, NOT A HOPE — AND F3 IS WHY

F3's deploy declared **45** visits and measured **44**, and its author could not
name the actor. A supervised session named it afterwards:
`worker/cleanup/stale-visits.ts`, a **BullMQ cron running daily at 03:00 UTC**
that appears in no crontab. It hard-deletes abandoned `QUEUED` drafts older than
24h, unaudited, by design.

**That cron cannot fire inside this deploy's window.** It runs at 03:00 UTC; this
deploy runs around 11:30–12:00 UTC, so the next sweep is ~15 hours away.

> **Row 4 is therefore falsifiable in a way F3's was not: if the visit count
> moves during this deploy, the daily sweep is NOT available as an explanation,
> and it is a finding.**

That is the difference between a row that can be explained away afterwards and
one that cannot — which is the whole product §2.5 is buying.
