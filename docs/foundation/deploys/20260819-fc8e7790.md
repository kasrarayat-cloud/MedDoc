## WHAT DIRECTION THIS CHANGE MOVES, BECAUSE IT DECIDES WHICH ROWS MATTER

F12-d's declaration had to say the predicate DRIVES WRITES, and that is still
true: `page.tsx:656-667` writes `emScoredCode` on render when the verdict is
true, `sign/route.ts:578` freezes `cptCodes.em.signedCode`, `rescore-em.ts:118`
writes `emStale`. So a rollback is not a clean inverse here either.

⚠️ **THIS CHANGE MOVES IN BOTH DIRECTIONS AT ONCE, AND THAT IS WHY BOTH KINDS OF
ROW ARE BELOW.** The counterweight widening is a **LOOSENING** — more label sets
read as an office visit, so fewer visits are suppressed. The `templateIsSilent`
fix is a **TIGHTENING** — more templates are silent, so the note body is
consulted more often and an operative body suppresses where it previously could
not. Ruling #123 calls over-suppression worse than the defect being fixed;
under-suppression puts an office E/M on an operative note, which is the defect
F12 exists to remove. **A presence-only declaration cannot detect either
mistake**, so rows 2, 4 and 6 are ABSENCE rows and they are the ones that
discriminate.
