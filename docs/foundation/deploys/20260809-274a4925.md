## 1. WHAT KIND OF DEPLOY THIS IS

**CODE-ONLY. B2 carries NO migration.** The applied-migration count must be
*identical* afterwards, not merely "all applied" — movement there would mean
something entered this deploy that nobody declared.

⚠️ **The one-line code-only declaration — *"nothing but the image ids"* — IS NOT
SUFFICIENT HERE.** B2 adds new *writers*: on the seven wired branches a vendor
failure now emits an `api_usage_events` row that would not have existed before.
This deploy CAN legitimately move a row count, so the declaration must name
which one and in which direction **in advance**, or the check degenerates into
"the number moved, and there is a reason available".

## 3. WHAT MUST **NOT** CHANGE

- **Clinical counts — visits 25, signed 16, patients 15.** A deploy that moves
  any of these has done something nobody asked for. Never omitted.
- **Applied migrations: 95.** B2 carries none.
- **`unitsSource='mock'` stays 0.** A mock row in production would mean the mock
  seam leaked — the exact regression B0-a exists to prevent, and one B2's new
  writers could have reintroduced had the helper hardcoded its provenance
  instead of mirroring the success path.
- **Existing rows are not rewritten.** `sum(totalCostUsd)` over the 1325
  pre-existing rows stays **$26.3556**. B2 adds rows; it edits none.
- **Co-tenants** — `app` 307 · `admin` 404 · `quillmd` 200 · `www` 200 ·
  `pro` 410.

## 4. WHAT MAY CHANGE, AND ONLY THIS

- **The image ids** for `transcribe-md` and `transcribe-md-worker`.
- **`api_usage_events` may GAIN rows, in exactly one new shape**: rows whose
  `metadata->>'outcome' = 'failed'`. Any new row without that marker is an
  ordinary success-path row, unrelated to B2.
- **`ai_calls` may gain rows** from ordinary traffic — including, new with B2, a
  row from `llm-mdm.ts`'s judge catch, which previously wrote none.

## 5. THE HONEST EXPECTATION, AND THE TRAP IN IT

**The most likely correct outcome is that NOTHING moves except the image ids.**

The wired branches fire only when a real vendor call fails, and production is
quiet.

⚠️ **THEREFORE A JUMP IN `outcome='failed'` ROWS IMMEDIATELY AFTER DEPLOY IS A
FINDING, NOT A SUCCESS.** It would not mean "B2 works"; it would mean the wiring
fires on a path that is not a vendor failure. **Zero new rows is the expected
reading and is NOT evidence that B2 is broken** — the behaviour is proven by the
gate, not by production traffic.

Writing that down now is what stops a disappointing zero being talked into a
problem, or a surprising jump being talked into a success.

**Judgement rule:** every row in §3 unchanged, and any `api_usage_events` growth
accounted for row-by-row against §4. **A difference that is not in this
declaration is THE FINDING**, whatever its explanation sounds like afterwards.

## 6. THE BACKFILL QUESTION IS CLOSED — RULING 79

The earlier draft of this declaration asked for a backfill ruling, on the
strength of a measurement taken while capturing this baseline: DISCOVERY §C.3
counted **44** failed-but-billed `ai_calls` rows on 2026-08-04; there are **42**
today, and rows leave that table only via its four deleters.

**The ruling was granted the same evening and it said no.** Ruling 79: the
durable meter's **provenance regime** begins at B0-a, and no pre-regime call is
ever **INSERTED** into it — a row minted today for a June call would assert
conformance with `unitsSource` (66), the no-trusted-default doctrine (67) and
the `requestId ⇒ vendor` CHECK (72), none of which existed when the call was
made.

⚠️ **The distinction is the whole ruling: backfilling a COLUMN onto rows the
meter already holds is correct and was done by B0-a itself** (1312 rows). What
is forbidden is inserting rows for calls the meter never recorded.

The evidence was preserved the other way —
`docs/qa-reports/failed-but-billed-ai-calls-extract-20260809.md`, all 42 rows,
cost-accounting fields only, **deliberately a document rather than a table
because a document cannot later be mistaken for a bill.**

⚠️ **And my attrition reasoning was corrected: the mean rate is the wrong
instrument.** `delete-account` is practice-wide and atomic, and **one practice
holds 24 of the 42 rows and 72% of the $1.50** — the loss is a step function,
not a drip.

**Nothing in this deploy touches any of it.** Recorded here only so the
declaration does not ship carrying a question that has been answered, or the
retracted instruction that came with it.
