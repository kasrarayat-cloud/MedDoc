  without a physician, the one thing F5-b promises it will not do

- migrations ≠ 104 → an unexpected migration shipped

  bundle (a `NEXT_PUBLIC_*` read surviving at runtime means it was not inlined)
- **STEP 0b absent from the log**, or reporting anything but idempotent → F9's
