## 1. WHAT KIND OF DEPLOY THIS IS

**CODE-ONLY. B2 carries NO migration.** The applied-migration count must be
*identical* afterwards, not merely "all applied" — movement there would mean
something entered this deploy that nobody declared.

⚠️ **But the one-line code-only declaration — *"nothing but the image ids"* — IS
NOT SUFFICIENT HERE, and saying why matters.** B2 adds new *writers*: on the
seven wired branches a vendor failure now emits an `api_usage_events` row that
would not have existed before. So this deploy CAN legitimately move a row count,
and the declaration must name which one and in which direction **in advance**, or
the check degenerates into "the number moved, and there is a reason available".

## 3. WHAT MUST **NOT** CHANGE

- **Clinical counts — visits 25, signed 16, patients 15.** A deploy that moves
  any of these has done something nobody asked for. This row is never omitted.
- **Applied migrations: 95.** B2 carries none.
- **`unitsSource='mock'` stays 0** in production. A mock row appearing would mean
  the mock seam leaked into prod — the exact regression B0-a exists to prevent,
  and one B2's new writers could have reintroduced had the helper hardcoded its
  provenance instead of mirroring the success path.
- **Existing rows are not rewritten.** `totalCostUsd` over the 1325 pre-existing
  `api_usage_events` rows stays `$26.3556`. B2 adds rows; it edits none.
- **Co-tenants** — `app` 307 · `admin` 404 · `quillmd` 200 · `www` 200 ·
  `pro` 410.

## 4. WHAT MAY CHANGE, AND ONLY THIS

- **The image ids** for `transcribe-md` and `transcribe-md-worker`.
- **`api_usage_events` may GAIN rows, in exactly one new shape**: rows whose
  `metadata->>'outcome' = 'failed'`. Any new row without that marker is an
  ordinary success-path row and is unrelated to B2.
- **`ai_calls` may gain rows** from ordinary traffic — including, new with B2, a
  row from `llm-mdm.ts`'s judge catch, which previously wrote none.

## 5. THE HONEST EXPECTATION, AND THE TRAP IN IT

**The most likely correct outcome is that NOTHING moves except the image ids.**

The wired branches fire only when a real vendor call fails, and production is
quiet. The 42 historical failed-but-billed calls accumulated over months — a base
rate of roughly one every few days — so a post-deploy window of minutes should
see **zero** new failure rows.

⚠️ **THEREFORE A JUMP IN `outcome='failed'` ROWS IMMEDIATELY AFTER DEPLOY IS A
FINDING, NOT A SUCCESS.** It would not mean "B2 works"; it would mean the wiring
fires on a path that is not a vendor failure. **Zero new rows is the expected
reading and is NOT evidence that B2 is broken** — the behaviour is proven by the
gate, not by production traffic. Writing that down now is what stops a
disappointing zero being talked into a problem, or a surprising jump being talked
into a success.

**Judgement rule:** every row in §3 unchanged, and any `api_usage_events` growth
accounted for row-by-row against §4. **A difference that is not in this
declaration is THE FINDING**, whatever its explanation sounds like afterwards.

### ✅ ANSWERED THE SAME EVENING BY RULING 79 — THE BACKFILL IS DECLINED

This section was written asking for a backfill ruling. **It was granted within
the hour, and it said no.** Recorded here rather than left as an open ask,
because a declaration carrying a retracted instruction is worse than one that
never raised it.

**Ruling 79: the durable meter's PROVENANCE REGIME begins at B0-a, and no
pre-regime call is ever INSERTED into it.** A row minted today for a June call
would assert conformance with `unitsSource` (66), the no-trusted-default
doctrine (67) and the `requestId ⇒ vendor` CHECK (72) — none of which existed
when the call was made, and one row later it would be indistinguishable from a
row that earned it.

⚠️ **The distinction is the whole ruling, and the naive phrasing forbids
something ALREADY SHIPPED.** Backfilling a *column* onto rows the meter already
holds is correct and was done by B0-a itself (1312 rows). What is forbidden is
**inserting rows for calls the meter never recorded.**

**The evidence was preserved the other way:**
`docs/qa-reports/failed-but-billed-ai-calls-extract-20260809.md` snapshots all
42 rows — cost-accounting fields only, `errorDetails` excluded as
may-contain-PHI (and non-null on 42 of 42 of exactly this population).
**Deliberately a document rather than a table, because a document cannot later
be mistaken for a bill.** Verified: 0 of the 42 fall on or after B0-a, so the
boundary separates a wholly legacy population and costs no meter row.

⚠️ **My attrition reasoning was also corrected: the mean rate is the wrong
instrument.** `delete-account` is practice-wide and atomic, and **one practice
holds 24 of the 42 rows and 72% of the $1.50** — so the loss is a step
function, not a drip. "Roughly one every few days" was the wrong shape for the
risk.

**The rows keep perishing and nothing stops that, by design** — the four
deleters are correct behaviour. The extract is a copy taken before the
originals go, not a rescue.
