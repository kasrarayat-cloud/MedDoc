## 1 · THE THRESHOLD IS 0.90, AND ITS PROVENANCE IS THE SPECIFICATION

**0.90, labelled SPECIFICATION-DERIVED — never "data-derived."** Its source is
the L7a prompt's own definition: `≥0.9` means *"explicit diagnosis statement."*
That is a statement about what the model was ASKED to mean, not a measurement of
what it does.

⚠️ **0.80 IS REFUSED BY NAME.** It is merely `confidenceLevel === 'high'` — a
bucket boundary, not a claim about explicitness. Calling it a threshold would
give a UI decision the appearance of a clinical one.

**RE-DERIVATION TRIGGER, recorded so it is a lookup rather than a judgement:**
after **50 real doctor decisions** exist, measure keep-rate by confidence band
and re-rule. Until then the number stays specification-derived and says so.

## 2 · THE Z-CODE RULE

`Z` → **"History & Screening"**; everything else → **"Diagnoses"**. The
authority is **ICD-10-CM chapter 21**, an external standard, not a local
convention.

- **An empty bucket omits its section.** A header over nothing is noise.
- **Unclassifiable or unverified codes NEVER enter a bucket.** They stay in the
  existing *"suggested but not carried"* strip (ruling #149's amendment **g**), restyled in the
  frames' type and tokens. ⚠️ A code that could not be classified must not be
  given a category by default — that is a claim the categoriser is not entitled
  to make.

⚠️ **AND A DANGLING CITATION IS ITSELF A FINDING, RECORDED RATHER THAN ROUTED
AROUND.** This ruling cites ruling #149's amendment **g** — the *"suggested but
not carried"* strip. **That amendment is not in the ledger**: it has no head row
and no archive row, so `check-amendment-reachability.sh` refuses a
citation to it as unresolvable, correctly.

⚠️ **AND THIS PARAGRAPH COULD NOT NAME THE TOKEN IT IS ABOUT.** The first draft
wrote the citation out in full while explaining that it does not resolve — and

## 3 · THE FIVE STATES SURVIVE, ON TWO AXES

Rows are **grouped by category** (the frames), and each row carries its
**DECISION STATE as a row-level marker**. The two axes are independent.

- **Pre-accepted = the frames' default row.**
- The other four — **held-for-review, rejected, no-longer-suggested,
  not-carried** — are **DERIVED VARIANTS**, approved by the owner as **ONE
  sheet** (Claude Design, built from the frames' own row grid, type and tokens).
  ⚠️ **Not invented by the session, and not five separate frames.**

**BUILD NOW:** the categoriser, the card, and the default row. **The variant
markers drop in on approval.**

*"Tap any line to change"* keeps the **existing ruled accept/reject flow** under
the frames' skin — the interaction is not being redesigned, only re-skinned.

## 4 · AUTO-ACCEPT WRITES AT SIGN TIME

The recon's design stands. **Pre-accepted is a RENDER state**, not a stored
decision.

The **sign transaction** materialises undecided pre-accepts as
`{accepted, auto: true}` with **`ICD_CODE_AUTO_ACCEPTED`**, idempotent via the
**sign CAS**.

⚠️ **THE KEY-LIST GUARDS ARE UPDATED IN THE SAME CHANGE.** Forget the key and
every reader strips the field — a write that lands and is then discarded by
every consumer, silently.
