## #189 — THE CONFIGURED MODEL ID IS VALIDATED AT STARTUP

**The fail-closed refusal is kept. Its TIMING moves.**

`shouldOmitTemperature()` refuses any model matching neither the stripped nor
the accepted family list, at three request-building sites. That refusal is
right: a wrong guess is a 400 on the clinical path or an undeclared change to
sampling.

**What is wrong is where it lands.** `TRANSCRIBE_MD_CLINICAL_MODEL` is read by
production code, and **every** clinical layer passes a temperature — L2 `0`,
L4 `0.2`, L6 `0.1`, L7a `0`, L7b `0`, L7e `0.3`, 04b `0`, 06b `0`. So a
one-character typo does not fail fast; it throws uncaught on the first clinical
request, **inside the pipeline, wearing the costume of a generation failure.**

> **A CONFIGURATION ERROR MUST NOT MASQUERADE AS A CLINICAL ONE.** The two are
> diagnosed by different people, in different places, at different hours.

**Small, armed, its own PR.**
