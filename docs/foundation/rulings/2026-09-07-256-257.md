not "six"**, all four §2.5 obligations; the **autonomous witness with its kill
control**; the **`pg_locks` instrument with its corrected control**.

Plus the **renumber** and the **duplicate-root-ruling gate**.

⚠️ **THE RATIFICATION NAMES THE CONTROLS, NOT THE FIXES, AND THAT IS
DELIBERATE.** Two of the instruments in this batch were false greens on their
first version — the `pg_locks` probe read a lock an earlier arm had taken, and
the previous witness could never say NO. **Both were caught by their own
controls.** An instrument without a control that has actually failed is a claim.

### ⚠️ TWO CITATIONS IN THIS BATCH DO NOT RESOLVE, AND ARE CORRECTED HERE RATHER THAN QUIETLY

**(a) `canon 137` DOES NOT EXIST.** A peer message cited it as the entry
recording the earlier renumber incident, and this session repeated that number in
**three commit messages now on `origin`**. The highest canon entry anywhere in
the repository at that moment was **134**; the entry meant is almost certainly
**132**. The peer's prose was accurate; its citation was not, and the citation is
the half that propagated.

**(b) THE RULING GRANTING THIS BATCH CITED `#250` FOR THE EXIT CONDITION.** That
rule — *a round finding only instrument defects, with those instruments repaired,
releases the draft* — is **#254**. It was `#249` before this lane renumbered, and
`#250`/`#255` is the sandbox-hygiene ruling. **The citation predates the
renumber, which is exactly the drift a renumber causes and exactly why it is
recorded rather than silently read as intended.**

## #257 — OWNER-RULING — ONE ROUND EACH, AND #254's RULE RELEASES THE DRAFT

**STANDING.**

1. **One round each**, on #1036 and #1018.
2. **#254's rule governs the outcome:** a round finding **only INSTRUMENT
   defects**, with those instruments **repaired**, **RELEASES THE DRAFT.**
3. Then **both ride the next composed deploy, with their rows.**

⚠️ **THE DISTINCTION IS JUDGED HONESTLY OR THE RULE IS WORTHLESS.** A finding is
a **CODE** defect if it changes what the product does to a doctor, a document or
a row. It is an **INSTRUMENT** defect if it changes only what the proof can SEE.
*"No arm can detect X"* is an instrument defect; *"X is wrong"* is a code defect;
**a finding that says both is a code defect.**

⚠️ **AND THE JUDGE IS THE PARTY WITH THE INCENTIVE.** This session wrote the code
and wants the draft released, and it is also the party classifying the findings.
The mitigation is that **the classification is stated per finding, in the
report** — not summarised — so the call is auditable by somebody who did not make
it.

**THE STALE SHARED TREE IS THE DEPLOY OWNER'S.** `/opt/meddoc-pro` is many merges
behind `main`; it fast-forwards at the next deploy act. **Not fixed here** — ten
peer processes are live on that tree and moving it under them is the hazard the
worktree discipline exists to prevent.
