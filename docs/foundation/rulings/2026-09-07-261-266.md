## #261 — OWNER-RULING — **A ROW THAT PRINTS A PATIENT'S NAME IS NEVER CERTIFIED BELOW THE FOLD**

**STANDING.**

> `bulkConfirmable` **excludes `visit_metadata`**, and that exclusion is a
> **RULING**, not a property of the current implementation.

**WHY IT IS A RULING AND NOT A DETAIL.** Bulk confirmation is a convenience: it
certifies a set of dispositions the doctor has not individually read, on the
argument that the machine's proposal is obviously right and the cost of
reviewing each is disproportionate. That argument holds for a concept like
`allergy.substance`. It does not hold for a field whose rendered output is
**the patient's own name**, because the failure mode is not a mis-mapped
concept — it is a document that confidently prints the wrong person.

⚠️ **THE HAZARD IS THAT THE EXCLUSION LOOKS LIKE A GAP.** A future session
tidying `bulkConfirmable` will find `visit_metadata` conspicuously missing from
a list its siblings are on, read that as an oversight, and close it. The
exclusion is therefore recorded where that session will look, with its reason
attached — the same move canon makes for every deliberate-looking-like-an-omission.

Related: **#247** (family 3 / `visit_record`), which is what makes
`visit_metadata` a fillable class at all.

## #262 — OWNER-RULING — **TWO OF YOUR THREE "BLOCKERS" ARE YOURS. THE DOOR EXISTS.**

**STANDING.**

> This lane certified and activated `939aa9ea` and ran its ten-row correction
> against production yesterday under a mutation declaration; Design/Admin ran
> #226 this morning. **Both records name how the act reached the database.**
> Use that door — and **make the wrapper the door if it is not yet.**

**THE MECHANISM ORDERED:**
`run-ruled-mutation.sh` **executes the declared act INSIDE the app container**,
as `deploy-prod.sh` already does for migrations — so **no session needs `.env`
and no session types a raw `exec`.** The shape to copy is `scripts/dc.sh` and
`scripts/reap-session-scratch.sh`: a named script whose grant is
`Bash(bash scripts/<named>.sh:*)`, because nothing after that prefix can change
which file runs.

**AND ONLY THEN, IF ANYTHING IS LEFT:** if ONE named permission rule is all that
still stands between the session and the act, **name it in one line** and the
owner grants it. **Nothing else is his.**

⚠️ **THE GENERALISABLE HALF, AND IT IS THE REASON THIS IS A RULING.** A session
that cannot perform an act has two available explanations — *the system forbids
it* and *I have not found the door* — and they are indistinguishable from the
inside. Escalating the first when the second is true costs the owner a decision
he should never have seen, and it costs it in the currency the standing-authority
directive was written to protect. **The record of a peer performing the same act
is the check**, and it is one grep.

## #264 — OWNER-RULING — **AGE NEVER GUESSES**

**STANDING.**

> Until `Practice.timezone` exists, the age injector **REFUSES** — blank, with a
> **named reason** — whenever the age would differ **anywhere in the
> UTC-12..UTC+14 window** of the visit instant.

**WHY A REFUSAL AND NOT A BEST GUESS.** A one-year-high age on a consult letter
is a **clinical error**, not a formatting blemish. The window is ±26 hours wide,
so for any patient whose birthday falls near the visit date there exists a real
timezone in which the computed age is different — and the server's own zone is
not evidence about the doctor's.

⚠️ **THE REFUSAL IS THE CONSERVATIVE ANSWER AND THE BLANK IS THE POINT.** A
blank field with a named reason is a question the doctor can answer in one
second. A confident wrong number is one he will not notice.

### BOARD FILING — **PRACTICE-TZ**

Filed to Design/Admin: **`Practice.timezone` (IANA)** — captured at signup,
editable, and **used by every visit-local-date derivation**, not only by age.
The refusal above retires the day it lands.

## #265 — OWNER-RULING — **MECHANISE THE RANGE; AND THREE SWEEP ARMS**

**STANDING.**

**(a) THE RANGE.** The declaration **derives** `production..HEAD` **from the
last judged deploy's rollback tag** — **never from `origin/main`**. **A TYPED
RANGE IS REFUSED.**

⚠️ `origin/main` answers *what has been merged*, which is not *what is running*.
The two agree most days, and the day they do not is the day the range silently
omits or invents commits — and a §2.5 declaration whose range is wrong is the
kind of evidence that looks exactly like evidence.

**(b) THE DUPLICATE GUARD.** Two duplicate root-ruling guards exist for one
property. **The first on `main` STAYS; the other lane DELETES its copy on
rebase** — canon 115.

**(c) THE SWEEP ARM.** **Every shell-out timeout in the test tree is bounded by
its own `it` budget.** Reference case `c4e778a2`: an arm **declared a 120s
budget and ran under a 5s one**, so its shell-out could never fail as a timeout
— it failed as a test timeout, with a message about the wrong thing.

**(d) `pro.quillmd.com` 410 IS THE INTENDED STEADY STATE — NEVER A PREFLIGHT
FIX.** Recorded so the next preflight does not re-diagnose it, and so nobody
"repairs" it.

## #266 — OWNER-RULING — **FILE: PROJECTOR-1 — BARE-KEYWORD TEMPORALITY**

**STANDING.**

> Bare-keyword temporality has **mis-stamped two clinical fields**: the **HPI
> header → `historical`**, and **"referred" → `planned`**.

**THE ARM ORDERED, AND ITS SCOPE IS THE WHOLE REGISTRY:** every registered
concept's **self-test sentence must project to its declared temporality**, armed
**over the whole registry** — not over a sample, and not over the two known
instances.

⚠️ **THE TWO NAMED FIELDS ARE THE SYMPTOM AND THE ARM IS THE POINT.** Fixing
two keywords closes two instances of a class that will keep producing new ones
every time a concept is added. A registry-wide arm turns "somebody noticed" into
"the next one cannot merge" — which is the difference between the two fixes.
