# Ruling #310 — 2026-09-09

## #310 — OWNER-RULING — FULL AUTONOMY THROUGH THE ADMIN ARC

**STANDING. Recorded at claim time, before any work was done under it.**

> **The Design/Admin lane runs the ADMIN arc END TO END without reporting in
> between. ONE consolidated report at the end:** what shipped, what was decided
> on the owner's behalf and why, what remains, and the preview URL of the design
> directions.

### THE SCOPE, IN ORDER — each with its adversarial pass and its §2.5 rows

1. Merge **#1078**; land **#1083** (ADMIN-4 backend) on a clean round.
2. **ADMIN-3a** — the `Practice` write path, *the one missing verb four rows and
   ruling #226 wait on* — then **#226's archive act** and **ADMIN-6** on top of
   it.
3. **#307** (meddoc-pro compose service removed) and **DMP-a** (the three
   merge-gating tests retargeted off `apps/meddoc-pro`); the **console PORT**
   (`system-admin` → `transcribe-md/admin`) as the shell's substrate;
   **DELETE-MEDDOC-PRO only after the port lands**.
4. **THE DESIGN** — three directions as HTML mockups via the `frontend-design`
   skill: **control room / ledger / instrument panel**, in three SEPARATE
   sub-sessions, with BAN lists and skeleton exclusions, production tokens known
   but breakable for exploration, published to the `_preview` path. Then the
   design-independent plumbing continues (ADMIN-2 subdomain, ADMIN-3 specialty,
   B8/B9 seats and promo) **while the shell waits for the owner's ONE pick**;
   the moment he picks, the shell and the money view are built into it.
5. **DEPLOY** — either lane may run a composed deploy under the full declaration
   discipline. **Never two in flight; the deploy-candidate marker arbitrates.**

### PRE-DECIDED — NEVER PAUSE ON THESE

| class | the standing answer |
|---|---|
| **(a) product questions** | the standing principles: *the chart owns the note; the tenant is billed; one predicate per rule; PORT not BUILD* |
| **(b) capability trades** | **fail closed, never strand, never withhold** |
| **(c) instruments** | fix the CAUSE — the union resolver, renumber the unlanded, sanctioned reclaims, build the missing wrapper |

⚠️ **THE THREE ROWS ARE NOT PERMISSION TO GUESS. They are pre-taken decisions**,
so a lane meeting one of these does not stop to ask a question whose answer is
already written down. A question that fits none of them is still surfaced.

### STOP — and only these

- **sudo**
- **a secret that exists in no container**
- **an irreversible production deletion no declaration covers**

⚠️ **NOTE WHAT IS *NOT* ON THAT LIST, BECAUSE IT IS THE POINT.** A red gate, a
failed round, a conflicting PR, a defect found in this lane's own work — none of
those is a stop. They are the work. The stop list is three things the lane
physically cannot decide, and everything else is decided and recorded.

### WHY THE REPORTING CHANGE IS SAFE, STATED SO IT IS NOT MISREAD

The proof burden is **unchanged**: every item still carries its adversarial pass
and its §2.5 rows, and the merge, push and deploy wrappers still refuse exactly
what they refused yesterday. What changes is the ROUTING of *narration*, not the
routing of *evidence* — the record is what makes an unasked question auditable,
and this ruling makes the record the deliverable rather than the running
commentary.
