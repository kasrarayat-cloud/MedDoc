## #285 — OWNER-RULING — TWICE IS THE PATTERN, AND `gate-f23` IS THE F30 CLASS

**STANDING. It corrects a session's own call.**

> **`gate-f23` has now red TWICE on unrelated diffs. Twice is the pattern.**

The session called the first red a flake, re-ran it, got green, and reported it
as confirmed-flaky. ⚠️ **A re-run that passes does not distinguish a flake from
a race; it is the outcome a race produces most of the time.** The evidence that
mattered was already on the record: an earlier session had counted **three
`printf | grep -q` instances in `prune-rollback-tags.sh`'s own loop** — the
**F30** class, already diagnosed and fixed once in
`check-retirement-conditions.sh`.

> **Fix those three with the F30 A/B probe, in its own small PR. Do not re-run a
> third time.**

⚠️ **AND THE TWO FAILURE DIRECTIONS ARE NOT THE SAME SEVERITY.** In the HELD
checks a false miss leaves `held=0` on an image a container is using, so the tag
joins the delete list — **a rollback tag deleted while in use**, which is the
artifact ruling **#137** exists to protect and is unrebuildable from `main` once
the tree has moved. In the reconciliation a false miss puts a reftag in BOTH
buckets, the partition stops adding up, and the script refuses before deleting
anything. One is a safety defect; the other is the observed symptom.
