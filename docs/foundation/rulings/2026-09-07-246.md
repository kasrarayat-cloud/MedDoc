## #246 — OWNER-RULING — **REGISTRY-1**: THE FOUR CONCEPTS THE ACCEPTANCE NEEDS, AS THEIR OWN CHANGE

**STANDING. The last change before 🟢.**

The eleven-row diff came back 🟡 with four rows blocked by one cause: the closed
concept registry has no term for them. Measured through `isRegisteredConcept`,
not by grep — `past_surgical_history`, `visit_metadata` (even bare),
`social_history.marital_status` and any total-time concept are all **REFUSED**.

Those four rows were never confirmation work. **REGISTRY-1 is the change that
makes them buildable, and it gets its own adversarial pass and its own deploy.**

### WHAT IT REGISTERS

| # | concept | what it needs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **`past_surgical_history`** | L3 extraction **+** projector **+** registry **+** synonyms |
| 2 | **`social_history.marital_status`** | an L3 **sub-concept** |
| 3 | **`visit_metadata.patient_name`**, **`.patient_age`**, **`.referring_physician`** | **record-derived**, carrying a *"from patient record"* **source flag** — and ⚠️ **the referring physician comes from the DICTATED fact, not the record** |
| 4 | **`total_time_minutes`** | an L3 field, **number** |

⚠️ **ROW 3 IS THE ONE THAT IS NOT LIKE THE OTHERS, AND ITS FLAG IS THE POINT.**
A value taken from the patient record did not come out of the doctor's mouth. The
whole fill path is built on cited `EncounterFact`s with an evidence quote, so a
record-derived value must be marked as such rather than dressed up as dictation —
otherwise the note asserts, in the doctor's voice, something he never said. **The
referring physician is deliberately excluded from that flag**: he *is* dictated,
and treating it as record-derived would be the same error pointed the other way.

### WHAT FOLLOWS IT, IN ORDER

**REGISTRY-1 (its own pass, its own deploy)** → **re-propose the ten `manual`
fields** onto the new concepts → **confirm** → **re-certify** → **the owner
records a fresh GI visit and exports it** → **the eleven-row diff on HIS export**
→ **🟢**.

⚠️ **THE ELEVEN-ROW DIFF IS RUN ON THE OWNER'S OWN EXPORT, AND THAT CLOSES A GAP
THIS SESSION NAMED.** The last diff had to be run against the
verification-encounter dictation because the Alvarez transcript does not exist on
this machine. A real recorded visit is the acceptance test the eleven rows were
always written for, and it is the owner's eyeball that settles it — the one class
of judgement no gate substitutes for.
