This ruling answers the three mismatches recorded in
`docs/design/T4-SIGN-DELIVER-FIGMA-SPEC.md`, each of which was measured from the
component and the schema rather than read off the frames.

## 1 · THE FIVE EHR CLIPBOARD FORMATS SURVIVE

**The frames' row is applied PER FORMAT.** A *"Copy to clipboard"* group, drawn
in the row skin, with the EHR picker inside it. **Nothing collapses.**

The frames draw one *"Copy note to clipboard"* row; the product has five —
Epic, Athena, Cerner, eClinicalWorks and plain text. Collapsing them would be an
accepted-capability loss: a doctor on Epic picks the Epic format, and one row
cannot express which.

> **The frames' ROW is the design. The four rows drawn are examples.**

## 2 · EMAIL SHIPS AS "EMAIL TO ME", AND THE SAFETY LINE IS THE ROW'S CAPTION

T4 ships the row as **"Email to me"** — the behaviour that exists — in the
frames' skin: `toEmail: doctorEmail`, a constant subject, and a **link, never an
attachment**, deliberately not `/visits/<id>`.

The email group's *"Don't use for patient communication"* line **stays, as the
row's caption.**

> ⚠️ **A LITERAL FRAME THAT DELETES A SAFETY INSTRUCTION IS NOT THE SPEC.**

That sentence is the generalisable half. The frames' flat `DELIVER TO` list had
no slot for a per-group warning, and building them literally would have removed
the one line that stops a doctor mailing a note to a patient. Fidelity to a
mockup does not outrank a safety instruction the mockup did not know about.

## 3 · "SEND TO REFERRING PHYSICIAN" IS ITS OWN PRODUCT ITEM — NOT A T4 RIDER

Filed on the board **right after ADMIN**. It is real new work, and every part of
it is a decision the frames do not contain:

| part | what |
|---|---|
| data | a `referringPhysician` field on the visit — **name + secure contact** |
| source | populated from the dictated **Referring MD** fact the custom-file batch already maps (row 7) |
| consent | an **explicit recipient confirmation** by the signing doctor |
| delivery | an **authenticated secure link — never an attachment** |
| audit | a row naming **recipient and purpose** (treatment disclosure) |

**Design pending the owner's frame for the recipient step.**

⚠️ **WHY IT COULD NOT RIDE T4.** There is no `referringPhysician` in
`schema.prisma` — a grep returns zero — so the drawn row has no data behind it.
And sending to a third party is a different **recipient** and a different
**disclosure** from emailing the signing doctor a link to their own note. That
is a PHI decision, not a label change.

## 4 · THE POST-STATE SHIFT IS ITS OWN CHANGE

The checkbox → action shift lands **after** the skin, as its own change.

> **"Two claims wearing one diff" is the right reason.**

Before signing a destination is a choice you accumulate and then commit; after
signing it is a thing you do now, repeatedly. That is a behaviour change the
frames draw deliberately — and a re-skin that also changes what a click DOES
cannot be reviewed as either one.
