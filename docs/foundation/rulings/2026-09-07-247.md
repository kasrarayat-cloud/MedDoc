## #247 — OWNER-RULING — **FAMILY 3 IS A NEW FACT CLASS, NOT A REUSE**

**STANDING.**

> **`sourceType` gains `'visit_record'`** — its own resolution status, its own
> validator arm, its own renderer allow-list entry, its own count bucket — and
> **every record-derived value carries it.**

**`withProcedureDateFact` MOVES ONTO IT AND STOPS CLAIMING
`encounter_evidence`.** That is not a tidy-up: today it injects the visit date
as a synthetic fact stamped `encounter_evidence`, so the frozen manifest records
a record value **as dictated**. It is the exact defect this class exists to
prevent, already shipped, and the class is not credible while its one precedent
keeps lying.

**It is PER-VISIT BY CONSTRUCTION.** `approved_practice_default` is
practice-wide and **stays refused**; a patient's name is not a property of a
practice.

> **PROVENANCE IS THE WHOLE POINT OF THE CLASS. The manifest says
> *"from the patient record."*** A fill path built on cited evidence must not
> let a record value borrow the doctor's voice.

### THE THREE MEMBERS, DECIDED INDIVIDUALLY

| member | decision |
|---|---|
| **patient name** and **age** | **TWO SEPARATE CONTROLS.** ⚠️ Acceptance row 3 is **four controls, not interpolation** — interpolation does not exist in this product and **will not be invented** for it. **Age is computed from the record's DOB at the visit date**, and is a **number**. |
| **referring physician** | **DICTATED ONLY.** An L3 field emitting concept `visit_metadata.referring_physician` with sourceType **`extraction`** — it is not record-derived and must not wear the new class. `classifyFieldSource` **stops pinning `/referring physician/` to `manual`**, and must also recognise **"Referring MD"**, the owner's actual label. |

### PHI — RULED, BECAUSE IT WAS ASKED

**A patient name in the VISIT-TIME fill manifest is the visit's own PHI, under
the same posture as the note it fills. Permitted there.**

**The CERTIFICATION artifact never contains a real name.** The existing
synthetic-evidence gate stays, **and gains an arm that a `visit_record` fact can
never reach a certification manifest.** The two artifacts have different
lifetimes and different audiences, and the rule follows the artifact rather than
the field.

### THE TWO CONSERVATIVE CALLS, RULED

1. **A `visit_metadata` mapping NEVER auto-confirms.** A control that prints a
   patient's name requires a human to have seen it.
2. **No fourth confirm act.** The existing confirm carries it, with the
   provenance shown — *"from the patient record."*

## THE ORDER

**families 1 / 2 / 4 (its own pass)** → **family 3 as its own change (its own
pass)** → **ONE composed deploy** → **re-upload the pinned bytes** (identical
`fieldId`s), **transfer the 43 certified dispositions**, **re-propose the ten**
under the new registry, **confirm, certify, activate** → **the owner records a
fresh GI visit and exports it** → **the eleven-row diff on HIS export** → **🟢**.

⚠️ **THE RE-UPLOAD IS THE ONLY ROUTE, AND IT WAS MEASURED RATHER THAN ASSUMED.**
A CERTIFIED compiled version's mappings cannot be changed by any path —
`updateTemplateTwinMappings`, `runTemplateTwinSyntheticTest` and
`certifyAndActivateTemplateTwin` all refuse `IMMUTABLE` — and
`TemplateTwinCompiledVersion.noteTemplateId` is `@unique`, so a second compiled
version cannot attach to the same note template. There is no de-certify path.
