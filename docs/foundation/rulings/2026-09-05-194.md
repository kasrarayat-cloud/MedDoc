# RULING #194 — THE KEEP HALF IS DELETED. THE MACHINE NEVER PRINTS AN UNDICTATED DEFAULT

**Owner ruling, 2026-09-05.** Supersedes the keep half of #191, #192 and #193.
Recorded at claim time, before the deletion.

## WHY — THE PREMISE WAS REFUTED, NOT JUST THE MECHANISM

Six falsifications of the mechanism, then one of the premise.

| # | formulation | how it died |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a text heuristic on `"Choose an item."` | blocked **28 of 28** real dropdowns; the English-only repair blocked all 28 again in fr/de/es |
| 2 | `w:showingPlcHdr`, the declared signal | a POPULATION FACT about two documents — this repo's own template carries none, and keep printed `Enter assessment` onto a note |
| 3 | guard below the kind branches | a date control lost `Date of procedure: 12 March 2026`; a select was overwritten |
| 4 | guard below the binding dispatch | a legacy FORMTEXT field's declared-keep content was DESTROYED while the manifest called it kept |
| 5 | keep restricted by `field.kind` | **skipping a write is itself a write** — the renderer writes `w14:val="0"` + `☐` for a null, so a pre-ticked box stayed TICKED: `Anticoagulated ☒` on the strength of an unrelated dictated headache |
| 6 | `narrative_text` as the fence | `inferTextKind` is a **LABEL REGEX** — a `w:sym` tick survives inside a "narrative" control, while a real ROS line reading *"…or weight loss"* is classified `measurement` and refused, **11 of 16** |

⚠️ **AND THEN THE PREMISE ITSELF.** The keep branch fires when
`conceptCandidates.length === 0` — when **no fact carries the field's approved
concept** — and concept equality is the only test performed anywhere. So
*"nothing dictated contradicted them"* is asserted precisely when the resolver
found no matching evidence at all: **the reassurance is always about the empty
set.**

**MEASURED.** A scaffold reading *"Examination of both lower extremities shows
no varicosities, no oedema and no ulceration"* was KEPT on a visit dictated as
*"large weeping ulcer over the left medial malleolus with gross bilateral oedema
and prominent varicosities"* — because that fact carried `chief_complaint`
rather than `exam.vascular`. The signed note asserted the patient had no
ulceration on an ulcer visit, and the banner reassured the doctor nothing
contradicted it.

⚠️ **AND BEFORE THE SLICE THAT FIELD RENDERED BLANK AND THE BANNER SAID
"1 still blank" — WHICH WAS TRUE.** The work converted a truthful blank into a
false denial plus a false reassurance. A system that can only match concepts
cannot know a clause was uncontradicted; it can only know nothing matched its
concept, which is a different and much weaker statement.

## THE RULING

**1 · `unmentionedPolicy` COLLAPSES TO `NULL | 'omit'`.**

| value | behaviour |
|---|---|
| **NULL** | the bounded clear, **counted as still blank** — the doctor is told |
| **`'omit'`** | the bounded clear, **NOT counted**, no prompt — the telehealth line |

**No `'keep'` value exists anywhere** — schema, resolver, renderer, proposer,
counts. **`default_kept` is retired from the proof union with its arms.**

**2 · #179 PART 4 IS AMENDED THE SAME WAY.** A recomposed ROS sentence asserts
polarity **only for symptoms actually dictated** — *"The patient reports
heartburn; denies nausea."* — and says nothing about unmentioned symptoms in its
scope. **Never "denies X" for an unasked X.**

**3 · ROWS 4/5 ARE REDEFINED.** Contradicted → the clause filled from evidence
with only dictated polarities. Wholly unmentioned → **BLANK, honestly counted**.
Row 6 (`omit`) flips as proven.

**4 · THE DOCTOR'S DEFAULTS BECOME A HUMAN ACT AT REVIEW** — filed for
Design/Admin as **REVIEW-DEFAULTS**, right after the batch's 🟢. On the pre-sign
review, every blank scaffolded control shows its compiled `scaffoldText` with an
**"Insert my default"** control (and "Insert all my defaults", with the list
shown). The click is the **doctor's assertion**, audited
(`DEFAULT_INSERTED_BY_DOCTOR`), and fills through the normal human-typed path.
**Custom-file supplies the resolver side:** `scaffoldText` carried in the IR
(already built, outside the hashes) and a **human-fill proof kind**.

> **Speed preserved; the claim is the doctor's.**

**5 · THE TRANSFER** declares `'omit'` on telehealth **only**; the sixteen
ROS/exam controls take **NULL**.

## WHAT SURVIVES THE DELETION, AND WHY

`scaffolded` and `scaffoldText` stay in the compiled IR, **outside
`structureSha256` and outside the mappings hash**, because REVIEW-DEFAULTS needs
exactly them: which controls carry a default, and what it says. They inform a
human's choice and never a render.

## ⚠️ AMENDMENT #194(a) — 2026-09-06 — `approved_practice_default` SPLITS BY CONCEPT CLASS

**Owner ruling, recorded by the Design/Admin session. APPENDED, never rewritten:
everything above stands exactly as it was written on 2026-09-05.**

### How this was found, because the route matters

While building REVIEW-DEFAULTS — **the doctor-facing half of this very ruling** —
the resolver turned out to carry a second, live path that prints a default with
**no per-visit human act at all**:

    resolver/index.ts:254-255
      const practiceDefault = practiceDefaultResolution(field, mapping, facts);
      if (practiceDefault) return practiceDefault;

It returns **before** evidence matching, gated only on
`mapping.practiceDefault !== null && mapping.status === 'certified'`
(`resolver/index.ts:194`). It has a full validated provenance shape —
`sourceType: 'approved_practice_default'`, its own fact and anchor builders, and
a thirteen-conjunct validator arm at `validation/index.ts:513-535`.

⚠️ **AND #194 NEVER MENTIONED IT.** Measured:
`grep -c "approved_practice_default\|practiceDefault"` against this file
returned **0** before this amendment. Ruling #191 came closest, refusing
`practiceDefault: ''` **as the policy mechanism** — *"that is a VALUE pretending
to be a POLICY"* — which distinguishes the two mechanisms without saying
anything about the value one.

**So on the plain reading of this ruling's own headline — *the machine never
prints an undictated default* — that path contradicted it.**

### THE RULING: IT IS NOT ONE ANSWER, IT IS TWO

The owner's answer splits it by **concept class**, and the split is the point.

**EXEMPT — ADMINISTRATIVE fields.** Identity, credentials, addresses,
boilerplate. `approved_practice_default` stands unchanged for these:

- **the certification-time approval IS the human act**, merely earlier than a
  click; and
- **no dictation can contradict them.** A referring physician's address is not
  the kind of thing a transcript disputes, so the failure this ruling exists to
  prevent — a machine sentence standing where the doctor's dictation should
  have been — cannot arise.

**A HOLE — CLINICAL fields.** A pre-approved clinical sentence printing on every
visit is **the same class as `keep`**, which this ruling deleted. That it was
approved once, at certification, does not make it dictated on THIS visit; and a
clinical statement is exactly the kind a transcript can contradict.

### WHAT THE HOLE OWES, FILED RATHER THAN FIXED HERE

1. **A gate REFUSES certifying a `practiceDefault` on a mapping that carries a
   `clinicalConcept`.** The refusal belongs at certification, where the approval
   is granted, rather than at fill time — refusing at fill would strand a
   document over a decision made weeks earlier.
2. **Any EXISTING clinical `practiceDefault` is reduced to the per-visit
   human-gated shape REVIEW-DEFAULTS builds** — the doctor still gets their
   wording, and the click is what puts it in the note.

Filed as its own row rather than folded into REVIEW-DEFAULTS: the two are
opposite operations — one **removes** a machine path, the other **adds** a human
one — and *two claims wearing one diff* is how a reduction gets shipped
unnoticed inside a feature.

### ⚠️ AND THE SPLIT DECIDED A DESIGN QUESTION IT WAS NOT ASKED

REVIEW-DEFAULTS' inserted-default fact must carry a `clinicalConcept`, because
`factMatchesMapping` returns **false** whenever the mapping has none
(`validation/index.ts:188`), `SOURCE_FACT_CONCEPT_MISMATCH` is unconditional,
and `POPULATED_WITHOUT_EVIDENCE` blocks a populated value that cites nothing —
so **there is no populated-with-evidence path for a concept-less field at all.**

Read alone that looks like a limitation. Under this split it is the correct
boundary: **administrative fields keep `approved_practice_default`, clinical
fields get the human-gated insert, and the concept requirement is exactly what
separates them.** One mechanism each, and the type system enforces the line.
