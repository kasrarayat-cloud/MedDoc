## #204 — THE DOOR CONSULTS THE PER-VISIT RESOLUTION

**STANDING. Supersedes the mechanism of `432bd450`, not its diagnosis.**

The offer and the door share **one per-visit predicate**, resolved through the
same entry the preview uses — `readAndPrepareTemplateTwinFill` — and **never a
static policy, and never a cache**.

⚠️ **THIS OVERTURNS A JUDGEMENT I MADE AND SHOULD NOT HAVE.** `432bd450` deleted
the door's static refusal outright and justified it on ruling #194's principle
(an insert is a human act, audited `DEFAULT_INSERTED_BY_DOCTOR`). The second
adversarial pass falsified **both halves** of that justification:

- **The door now ADMITS acts the offer REFUSES**, on three input classes. It
  writes the `TemplateTwinFieldOverride` row and emits an audit row asserting a
  claim the doctor never made.
- **The audit row cannot evidence the case it was used to justify.** Changing
  the emitted action from `DEFAULT_INSERTED_BY_DOCTOR` to something else leaves
  the whole gate green, so nothing binds the audit to the argument resting on it.

**THE DOOR GETS EXECUTABLE COVERAGE.** Arms that DRIVE
`insertScaffoldDefaultAction`; **deleting a guard must go RED.** Measured
tonight: the door's guards can be deleted wholesale and **843 tests stay
green**, while the control — deleting the offer's clause instead — reds four
arms. The harness is not broken; it has **zero executable coverage of the
door**, and three whole-file string reads standing in for it.

**THE WEAK ARMS ARE REPLACED. No body-slices, no literal-spelling regexes.**
Derive from the function, or use the parser. Both failure directions were
demonstrated:

| the door's refusal via a local variable | the assertion was a literal-spelling regex |
| the mirror's first guard replaced by a literal | the matrix holds `field.kind` constant |

and the same arms produce **false REDS**: a loose `/*` block comment survives
the line filter, and changing **one word inside a comment** reds the
derived-dimension arm.

**`KEY_VERSION` v7 RIDES #1028.** #1028 merges first — its three rulings are

only then may a commit cite #203. The pre-commit hook already refuses the cite;

## #205 — THE ADMIN-5 HARNESS MUST GENUINELY RACE

**STANDING. The mechanism stands on its reasoning; it is UNPROVEN until the
harness races, and #200's body must say so.**

`race()` builds a heredoc whose terminator line carries the redirect and the
`&`, so bash swallows everything after it — the second script included — into
the first heredoc's body. Verified three ways: CI's empty capture strings, a
bash reproduction emitting *"here-document delimited by end-of-file"*, and a
psql reproduction in which **both scripts' statements execute sequentially in
ONE session, outside a transaction**.

**THE REQUIRED SHAPE:**

- **each script in its own FILE**;
- **two psql PROCESSES launched with `&` and awaited**;
- **outputs captured to files.**

⚠️ **AND THE NEGATIVE CONTROL MUST FAIL UNDER SEQUENTIAL EXECUTION.** Reaching
zero owners is **not** a witness of overlap — the unguarded UPDATE is
unconditional, so sequential execution reaches zero too. The control's own
comment claims that reaching zero proves the sessions overlapped; **it proves
nothing of the kind, and it passed all night while nothing raced.** An
**OVERLAP WITNESS** is required: both backends observed alive in
`pg_stat_activity` inside the window, or an advisory-lock choreography that
forces the interleaving.

**THE PR BODY'S MATRIX IS REPLACED BY THE HARNESS'S OWN OUTPUT.** The table in
#1026 was lifted from the author's commit message and presented as measurement
by a session that had not re-derived it. It cannot have come from this harness.

## #206 — THE REAP WRAPPER REFUSES TO REMOVE ANOTHER SESSION'S LIVE LENS SANDBOX

**STANDING. Filed to the custom-file session as a board item.**

**Two lens trees were destroyed mid-pass tonight.** Both lenses noticed, rebuilt
read-only trees with `git archive`, verified `@meddoc/*` resolved locally, and
recorded the interruption in their provenance notes — which is the only reason
their results are usable. That is the agents behaving better than the tooling.

**THE RULE:** a lens sandbox carries a **marker file naming its owner session id
and pid**, and the reap wrapper **refuses to remove a sandbox whose owner is
still alive**, alive-checked rather than assumed.

⚠️ **THE SHAPE IS THE ONE THIS PROGRAMME KEEPS MEETING:** three sessions share
one directory and one `refs/heads`, and `git worktree` isolates the CHECKOUT and
never the namespace. A reaper that cannot tell whose tree it is holding is the
same class as a merge that cannot tell whose runs it is cancelling — and the
answer is the same, a declaration or an owner, checked rather than inferred.
