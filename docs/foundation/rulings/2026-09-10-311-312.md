## #312 — DECIDED UNDER #311(a)/(b)/(d) — THE TEN RE-PROPOSED ROWS ARE CONFIRMED, AND ONE IS NOT CONFIRMED AS PROPOSED

**STANDING. A product decision made on the owner's behalf, recorded before the
act that carries it out.**

The re-issue act (judged 11 of 11) left compiled version `d2120cdd` with **43
rows CERTIFIED by transfer and 10 re-proposed**. The judgement's finding was
that all ten came back proposed as model-filled where they had been `manual`, and
it offered a recommendation *"not taken"*: keep eight of them `manual`.

**That recommendation is declined, and the reason is the owner's own acceptance
test, not a preference.** Rows 2, 3, 7, 8 and 9 of the eleven-row acceptance ask
for exactly these fills: past surgical history as its own concept, `{patient}` /
`{age}` from the record, Referring MD / Patient / Age filled, **total minutes 45**,
and marital status through a `social_history` sub-concept. Ruling **#246
(REGISTRY-1)** registered these concepts *so that* these fields could fill, and
**#247** built the `visit_metadata` class with three separate provenances. Keeping
them `manual` would certify a form that fails five of its own acceptance rows by
design.

The recommendation was right about one thing, and that part holds: **a billing
quantity and an attestation must never be invented.** Under `extraction` they are
not. An extraction field fills only from a cited, dictated fact with its evidence
quote. If nothing was dictated, it takes the bounded clear and is counted as
blank. *Silence is illegal* and *the machine never prints an undictated default*
are both satisfied, because the model fills nothing that was not said.

### ⚠️ TELEHEALTH — THE ONE ROW NOT CONFIRMED AS PROPOSED, AND WHY IT CANNOT KEEP ITS OLD `omit` EITHER

- **The proposal cannot be certified as it stands.** `extraction` with no
  concept is refused by the writer (`certified && !clinicalConcept &&
  sourceType !== 'manual'`), and no registered concept exists for a telehealth
  consent attestation. Registering one is its own change, pass and deploy, and
  the standing rule is that an unmappable field is `manual`.
- **The certified predecessor's `manual` + `omit` is no longer writable.**
  `unmentioned-policy.ts`'s ladder returns `manual_source_not_filled` for any
  `manual` field, so `canOmitWhenUnmentioned` is false, and the writer (#1018)
  refuses an `omit` the resolver could never read. On `18a7ae19` that `omit` is a
  stale column value from before the writer guard. The resolver reaches
  `manual_source_not_filled` first, so the value has never had an effect.
- **So: `manual`, no concept, no policy.** The page is identical to the
  predecessor's, because a `manual` scaffolded control resolves `not_mentioned`
  and takes the bounded clear, so the consent sentence is **never printed by the
  machine**. The difference is the count. The line is now counted *still blank*
  instead of *omitted*, and the doctor inserts it at review through
  REVIEW-DEFAULTS (`DEFAULT_INSERTED_BY_DOCTOR`, audited). **A consent is an
  attestation, and the doctor's click is the attestation.** That is #311(b)'s
  human-fill row, and acceptance row 6 is satisfied on the page, where the line
  is omitted whenever it is unsupported.

### ⚠️ AMENDMENT, 2026-09-10 (BEFORE LANDING) — THE REFUTATION PASS OVER THE CONFIRM DOOR BROKE FOUR OF THIS RULING'S CLAIMS

**The wording above is kept, not rewritten.** Every item below was reproduced
against the code and read-only production data before this was written.

1. **The telehealth remedy DOES NOT EXIST.** The section above says the doctor
   inserts the consent line at review through REVIEW-DEFAULTS. They cannot.
   `scaffoldDefaultOfferable` returns false for a row with no concept
   (`clinician-override.ts`, *"the fill declines forever without a concept"*),
   and the overlay declines a stored insert with `no_approved_concept`. **So
   under `manual` the consent line is never printed, by the machine or by the
   doctor.** That is **exactly** the behaviour of the certified form that is
   active today (`manual`, no concept), so the confirm loses nothing that works
   now. But the claim was false, and row 6's *supported* half (print the line
   on a telehealth visit) has no path at all. **FILED as TELEHEALTH-CONSENT:**
   either a registered concept plus an extractor, or a REVIEW-DEFAULTS insert
   for concept-less `manual` scaffolds. The decision stands, because no
   alternative prints only what was said.
2. **"An extraction field fills only from a cited, dictated fact" held only by
   PROMPT.** `encounter-facts` emits a fact as `direct` evidence even when its
   quote cannot be found in the transcript: the offsets fall back to null and
   the fact is kept. For two BILLING lines that is not acceptable. **The two
   Total-time rows are therefore confirmed together with #314**, a structural
   grounding guard: a `total_time_minutes` fact must be located in the
   transcript, and its quote must state the value. **Activation does not run
   until #314 is deployed.** The general gap, for every concept, is measured
   and filed separately. Its blast radius is too wide to change blind.
3. **Past Surgical History ×3 prints the whole list in every bullet.** The same
   is already true on the ACTIVE form for Past Medical History ×3, Impression ×4
   and Plan ×5 (measured on its certified proof), and it fails acceptance
   **row 1**. **#313 (distribution, one item per bullet) is therefore a
   precondition of activation**, not a follow-up.
4. **The writer stamps approval on all 53 rows, not ten.** The 43 transferred
   rows carry **no** `approvedById` today (measured: 0 of 43). The writer sets
   the owning doctor and the act's time on every row of the set it certifies.
   The door writes **one aggregate audit row** saying 43 of the 53 were carried
   over from the transfer, so their new stamp is attributable and does not read
   as ten more confirmations.

   ⚠️ **A verifier called this BLOCKING and proposed changing the shared writer**
   to stamp approval only on a row that MOVES into CERTIFIED. **Declined, with
   the reason on the record.** The writer's stamp means *"this set of mappings
   was approved, by this doctor's authority, at this time"* — it is the set that
   certification binds to (`approvedMappingsSha256`), and the 43 carried no stamp
   at all, so nothing is overwritten. The false-record risk the verifier named is
   real only if the stamp goes unexplained. It is explained three times: in this
   amendment, in the declaration's delta, and in the aggregate audit row.
   Changing the one writer every panel save goes through is a wider clinical-path
   change than this act needs. It would get its own pass, and it is filed.

Two defects in the door itself were also found and fixed before any act. The
drift guard compared seven attributes, and an unjudged `blankPolicy` /
`approvedSynonyms` edit would have been certified; it now compares the WHOLE
proposal, and the gate derives its drift matrix from the schema's own keys.
And a failed audit insert ended green; the door now reads its audit rows back,
refuses on a shortfall, and `--reissue-audit` writes only what is owed.

### ⚠️ AMENDMENT 2, 2026-09-10 (BEFORE LANDING) — ROUND TWO DELETED THE RECOVERY DOOR ROUND ONE'S FIX HAD ADDED

The first amendment's fix for a silently failed audit insert added a
`--reissue-audit` door. **A fix that adds a path earns another round, and that
round broke it.** Driven end to end on a state it could not tell apart (the
doctor confirming the same ten rows himself), it wrote eleven rows attesting
that **#311(d)'s hands** had confirmed rows the doctor confirmed. That is a
false record of exactly the question the audit exists to answer. It also
compared four attributes, reopening the partial-compare hole the first
amendment had closed.

**It is DELETED, not patched.** A recovery that cannot tell who acted cannot be
trusted to say who acted. The door now:
- writes its audit rows only **after reading the write back**, so the
  aggregate row carries the resulting set sha and certified count;
- checks that **the writer's own set-level row** exists with that sha;
- **exits 1 on any shortfall.** The judgement records it as the finding, and
  any recovery is its own declared act, written after reading what happened.

The round's other lens found **nothing** in the whole-set premise or the drift
compare, and it recomputed `dd4dc378…` from the real rows itself. **This change
only removes and narrows paths, so, by #304's exit rule, it is fixed and made
ready without a further round.**
