statement about what the product IS. It supersedes the derive-a-control-twin approach
(#320/#322/#323/#324/#325 and the pdf-native anchor render) for **prose Word and flat
PDF** uploads. It does NOT touch the DOCX-with-controls twin (that path is KEPT).

## Proof (2026-09-16, no deploy) — GO

The owner's colonoscopy PDF (`a06159ece48c`, the certified source) and the colon
`.docx` were run through the pipeline end-to-end against a self-hosted CommonForms
0.2.1 / FFDNet-L (ONNX/CPU). Result: field detection matches the visible blanks,
fills land in-box, and the page is pixel-identical outside the boxes on both paths.
Full evidence, counts, and slice plan: `docs/foundation/one-pipeline-proof-20260916.md`.

⚠️ Migration finding recorded here so the next session does not re-derive it: a
**Word-with-controls** form must NOT be flattened into stage 2 — its (empty) controls
flatten to nothing and the blanks vanish; Gotenberg instead carries content controls
through as AcroForm fields, so stage 2 for such a form is "use the native fields", and
FFDNet detection is for **flat PDF and prose Word** (visible blanks). This is why the
Word-with-controls path is kept until each form is migrated deliberately.
