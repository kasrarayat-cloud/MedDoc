## #225 — OWNER-RULING — C SHIPS. THE WINDOW IS CLOSED BY **SEQUENCING**, NOT ACCEPTED OPEN.

**STANDING. Supersedes the framing of the escalation, not its measurement.**

The session measured, correctly and with a control drawn from the pre-change
commit, that #216 moves `structureSha256` for the owner's GI template — the old
compiler reproduces production's stored `e9acc158…` exactly, the new one gives
`169fdf2d…` — and that `renderer.ts:assertSourceMatchesIr` re-compiles on every
fill, so the three GI compiled versions would begin failing `SOURCE_IR_MISMATCH`
and degrade **silently** to the styled export.

It then offered the owner a binary: accept the loss, or version the compiler.

> **⚠️ THE THIRD OPTION IS THE ONE TAKEN, AND IT IS NOT A COMPROMISE: THE WINDOW
> IS NOT ACCEPTED OPEN, IT IS CLOSED BY THE ORDER OF THE ACTS.**

### WHAT THAT MEANS CONCRETELY

**The deploy carrying #216 is immediately followed — SAME SESSION, SAME
MAINTENANCE ACT — by:**

1. **transfer/certify of the 53-control form**, recompiled under the new
   compiler and **re-pinned by DOCUMENT sha `ecb81703…`**. `structureSha256`
   moving is **expected and declared**, not a surprise to be discovered
   afterwards — which is the whole difference between a migration and an
   incident.
2. **ACTIVATION** of that form as the owner's GI template.

**There is no interval in which a degraded template is the active one and nobody
is looking.** The loss the escalation described requires a gap, and the ruling
removes the gap rather than paying for it.

### THE DEPLOY'S JUDGEMENT GAINS A ROW

> **"the active GI template fills end-to-end" — measured AFTER activation.**

⚠️ **THIS IS THE POINT, AND IT IS ABOUT THE FAILURE'S SHAPE, NOT ITS SIZE.** The
degradation `SOURCE_IR_MISMATCH` produces is **silent**: `native-export` catches
it and hands the doctor the generic styled export with only a `console.warn`.
A silent failure is exactly the kind a deploy judgement cannot catch by watching
counts, so the judgement asserts the BEHAVIOUR instead. **The degradation cannot
survive the act unnoticed**, because the act ends by exercising the thing that
would be degraded.
