## #314 — A TOTAL TIME IS GROUNDED BEFORE IT BECOMES A FACT

**STANDING.**

> **An L3 `total_time_minutes` entity becomes an `EncounterFact` only when
> (1) its quote is in the transcript, verbatim; (2) the quote states exactly
> ONE minute quantity, equal to the value, as digits or spelled out; and
> (3) the quote says it is a total — "total" or "spent". Otherwise there is
> NO fact.**

### WHY — MEASURED, NOT FEARED

`total_time_minutes` fills two BILLING lines on the owner's consult form
(controls 70 and 71). The refutation pass over the confirm door (#312, PR #1098)
demonstrated both of these, and each was independently verified:

| input | before #314 |
|---|---|
| transcript *"I spent twenty minutes reviewing outside records and twenty five minutes with the patient."*, with L3 returning `{minutes: 45}` | **both lines print 45**, `valid=true`, zero blocking |
| transcript *"Chief complaint: dysphagia."*, with L3 returning `{minutes: 60, quote: "We spent an hour together."}` (a quote that is **not in the transcript**) | **both lines print 60**, `valid=true`, zero blocking |

The only thing standing between a model's arithmetic and a billing line was a
sentence in the L3 prompt. `projectEncounterFacts` keeps a fact whose quote
cannot be located: the offsets fall back to null and the fact is still
`direct`. The standing principle is that the machine never prints an undictated
value, and a total nobody stated is exactly that.

### WHAT A REFUSAL DOES

**It is a blank, never a guess.** With no fact, both controls take the bounded
clear and are **counted as still blank**, so the doctor is told and types the
figure. The refusal reason is logged as a fixed token, with no quote and no
number, so the log is not a PHI surface.

⚠️ **THE STRICTNESS IS ONE-SIDED, AND DELIBERATE.** *"Total 45 minutes,
including 20 minutes of counselling"* is refused (two quantities). *"An hour"*
is refused (no minute quantity, and no conversion is attempted). A blank the
doctor fills is recoverable. A wrong billing figure on a signed note is not.

### WHAT IT DOES NOT COVER — FILED, NOT GUESSED AT

**The same ungrounded-quote gap exists for every concept.** Widening this rule
to all of them would blank fields across every visit without a measurement of
how many quotes L3 paraphrases today. That measurement comes first, as a filed
item (UNGROUNDED-QUOTES). **#314 covers the one concept that is a billing
quantity**, because that is where the harm is concrete and the rule is narrow.

### ITS PLACE IN THE SEQUENCE

**A precondition of activating the re-issued 53-control form.** #312 confirms
both Total-time rows as `extraction`, and the amendment to #312 makes activation
wait for #314 to be deployed.

### THE RULE, VERSION 2

> **An L3 `total_time_minutes` entity becomes an `EncounterFact` only when every
> step below holds. Otherwise there is NO fact.**

1. **Located.** The quote and the context are both normalised: whitespace runs
   become one space, text is lowercased, and curly apostrophes become `'`. The
   quote must occur at a **word boundary at both ends**. Every such occurrence
   is judged, and every one must pass.
2. **The sentence, not the quote.** For each occurrence, take the sentence or
   sentences of the context that it spans. A sentence ends at `.` `;` `!` `?`
   and at every line break, except that a `.` followed by a digit is a decimal
   point. Steps 3–5 are applied to that text. The model chooses the quote; it
   does not choose the sentence.
3. **Quantities are counted as occurrences, never as a set.** Each of these
   refuses, with no conversion attempted: any hour quantity, any seconds
   quantity, any decimal or clock time, and any range (`N-M`, N to/or/through M,
   between N and M). The sentence must then state exactly **one** minute
   quantity, as a number directly followed by its unit, equal to the value.
   Two other shapes also count as quantities, but as ones nobody can read: a
   number kept apart from its unit ("45 or more minutes", "45 face-to-face
   minutes"), and a number placed after the unit ("minutes: 45", "45 minutes 30").
4. **No negation and no past-visit marker in the sentence.** Negation means no,
   not, never, without, none, nor, neither, cannot, or any `n't`. A past-visit
   marker is last, previous or prior followed by visit, encounter, appointment,
   session, consult, consultation or time.
5. **An explicit total construction, tied to that one quantity:**
   `total [visit|encounter|face-to-face] time [spent] [today] [was|of|is|:] N minutes`,
   `a total of N minutes`, `total [of] N minutes`, `N minutes [in] total`,
   `time spent [was|is|:] N minutes`. Bare "spent" is not a total marker.
   Neither is "total" modifying another noun, whether it comes before the
   quantity ("total cholesterol") or after it ("45 minutes total cholesterol").

Every refusal is one of fifteen fixed tokens:
- `quote_empty`
- `minutes_not_a_positive_integer`
- `quote_not_in_transcript`
- `quote_splits_a_word`
- `hour_quantity_stated`
- `seconds_quantity_stated`
- `quantity_not_an_integer`
- `quantity_is_a_range`
- `no_minute_quantity_stated`
- `several_minute_quantities_stated`
- `minute_quantity_unreadable`
- `stated_quantity_differs_from_value`
- `negation_in_sentence`
- `past_visit_marker`
- `not_stated_as_a_total`

The log carries the token only, with no quote and no number. **A refused total
projects exactly as if L3 had returned no total key**, and deep equality
asserts it, including against a quote shared with facts projected before and
after it.

## AMENDMENT 2 — 2026-09-10 · A LINE BREAK DOES NOT END A SENTENCE

**Appended; nothing above is rewritten.** The amendment above says a sentence
ends at `.` `;` `!` `?` *"and at every line break"*. **That rule was measured
unsafe before it shipped.** The negation or past-visit marker sat on the line
above the total, so both of these grounded as 45:

    "This was not⏎a total of 45 minutes."
    "Last visit,⏎total time was 45 minutes."

**Decided: a sentence ends at `.` `;` `!` `?` only.** A line that carries its
own terminal punctuation still ends its sentence, so *"No fever.⏎Total time 45
minutes."* grounds. An unpunctuated line joins the next, so a negation above an
unpunctuated total refuses it. The cost is one-sided and deliberate: an
unpunctuated list note with an unrelated *"no"* above the total becomes a blank
the doctor fills. A blank is recoverable. A wrong billing figure on a signed
note is not.

### THE DECISION — THE ERRORS ARE ASYMMETRIC, SO THE RULE IS AN ALLOWLIST

Every accept-side finding was the same defect: v2 accepted any sentence that
*contained* a total construction and tried to refuse the bad ones with word lists
(hours, seconds, ranges, negations, past visits). Every list had a missing member
and every construction accepted words before or after it. A lexicon of refusals
cannot close that, because the next counterexample is always one unlisted word
away.

A wrong billing quantity on a signed note cannot be recovered. A blank with a
named reason can: the doctor sets the control at review. **So the rule flips from
a refusal lexicon to an ANCHORED ALLOWLIST.** A sentence grounds only when the
whole of it is one short canonical form, and everything else is a blank.

### THE RULE, VERSION 3

> **An L3 `total_time_minutes` entity becomes an `EncounterFact` only when every
> step below holds. Otherwise there is NO fact.**

1. **Located.** The quote occurs in the context at a word boundary at both ends
   (both normalised: whitespace runs become one space, lowercase, curly
   apostrophes become `'`). Every occurrence is judged.
2. **One sentence.** Each occurrence lies inside exactly one sentence. `;` `!`
   `?` end a sentence. A `.` ends one **only** when the next non-whitespace
   character is an uppercase letter, or there is none. A line break alone does
   not (amendment 2 stands). So "1 hr. 15", "approx. 45", "prev. visit" and "4.5"
   never split.
3. **The whole sentence is canonical.** The sentence is normalised as above, a
   hyphen between two number words becomes a space, and one trailing `.` is
   stripped. A sentence ending in `?` or `!` is `not_a_statement`. Otherwise the
   **entire** sentence must match one canonical form, or it is
   `not_stated_as_a_total`. Its one quantity must parse to a positive integer
   (`minute_quantity_unreadable` otherwise) equal to the value
   (`stated_quantity_differs_from_value` otherwise).
4. **Not retracted.** The sentence after each occurrence must not begin with
   *correction, strike that, scratch that, sorry, no wait, wait, actually, i mean,
   make that, rather* or *no,* (also a bare *No.*). Otherwise
   `corrected_in_next_sentence`.
5. **One total.** Every sentence of the whole context that matches a canonical
   form states the same minutes as the value. Otherwise `several_totals_stated`.

### THE CANONICAL FORMS

`Q` is exactly one quantity: `N (minutes|minute|mins|min)`, where `N` is a plain
integer of digits (an attached unit, "45min", is allowed) or a run of number
words that `parseSpokenCardinal` reads. Nothing may sit between `N` and the unit.
The list is exported as `CANONICAL_TOTAL_FORMS`; each form has a witness sentence
that it alone matches, and a mutation that deletes it.

```
F1        (the )?total (visit |encounter |face-to-face )?time( spent)?( today)?( (was|is|of))?( ?[:,])? Q
F2        (the )?total time spent (with the patient|on this visit|on this encounter)( today)?( (was|is))?( ?[:,])? Q
F3        (i |we )?spent a total of Q( (with the patient|on this visit|on this encounter))?( today)?
F4        (a )?total( of)?( ?[:,])? Q
F5        (the )?time spent( today)?( (was|is))?( ?[:,])? Q
F6        Q( in)? total( time)?
F6-spent  Q spent( in)? total
F7        a total time of Q (was )?spent (on this encounter|on this visit|with the patient)(, including T)?
```

`T` in F7 is letters, apostrophes, spaces and commas only, with no number word and
no time-unit word. F7 exists so that the certification encounter's own sentence,
*"A total time of forty five minutes was spent on this encounter, including
counselling and coordination of care."*, grounds. The arm imports that sentence
from `verification-encounter.ts`; it never copies it. "face to face" also matches
when it is hyphenated.
