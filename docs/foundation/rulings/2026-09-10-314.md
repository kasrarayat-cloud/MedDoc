## #314 — A TOTAL TIME IS GROUNDED BEFORE IT BECOMES A FACT

**STANDING.**

> **An L3 `total_time_minutes` entity becomes an `EncounterFact` only when
> (1) its quote is in the transcript, verbatim; (2) the quote states exactly
> ONE minute quantity, equal to the value, as digits or spelled out; and
> (3) the quote says it is a total — "total" or "spent". Otherwise there is
> NO fact.**

### WHY — MEASURED, NOT FEARED

`total_time_minutes` fills two BILLING lines on the owner's consult form
(controls 70 and 71). The refutation pass over the confirm door (#312, PR #1098)
demonstrated both of these, and each was independently verified:

| input | before #314 |
|---|---|
| transcript *"I spent twenty minutes reviewing outside records and twenty five minutes with the patient."*, with L3 returning `{minutes: 45}` | **both lines print 45**, `valid=true`, zero blocking |
| transcript *"Chief complaint: dysphagia."*, with L3 returning `{minutes: 60, quote: "We spent an hour together."}` (a quote that is **not in the transcript**) | **both lines print 60**, `valid=true`, zero blocking |

The only thing standing between a model's arithmetic and a billing line was a
sentence in the L3 prompt. `projectEncounterFacts` keeps a fact whose quote
cannot be located: the offsets fall back to null and the fact is still
`direct`. The standing principle is that the machine never prints an undictated
value, and a total nobody stated is exactly that.

### WHAT A REFUSAL DOES

**It is a blank, never a guess.** With no fact, both controls take the bounded
clear and are **counted as still blank**, so the doctor is told and types the
figure. The refusal reason is logged as a fixed token, with no quote and no
number, so the log is not a PHI surface.

⚠️ **THE STRICTNESS IS ONE-SIDED, AND DELIBERATE.** *"Total 45 minutes,
including 20 minutes of counselling"* is refused (two quantities). *"An hour"*
is refused (no minute quantity, and no conversion is attempted). A blank the
doctor fills is recoverable. A wrong billing figure on a signed note is not.

### WHAT IT DOES NOT COVER — FILED, NOT GUESSED AT

**The same ungrounded-quote gap exists for every concept.** Widening this rule
to all of them would blank fields across every visit without a measurement of
how many quotes L3 paraphrases today. That measurement comes first, as a filed
item (UNGROUNDED-QUOTES). **#314 covers the one concept that is a billing
quantity**, because that is where the harm is concrete and the rule is narrow.

### ITS PLACE IN THE SEQUENCE

**A precondition of activating the re-issued 53-control form.** #312 confirms
both Total-time rows as `extraction`, and the amendment to #312 makes activation
wait for #314 to be deployed.
