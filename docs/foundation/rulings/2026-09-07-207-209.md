## #207 — OWNER-RULING — `#179(6)` HAS CONTENT, AND THE PROJECTOR IS WRONG FIRST

**STANDING. Supplies the content this session recorded as absent, and supersedes
the "no recorded content" framing of `docs/foundation/board/179-REPAIR-OWNER-QUESTIONS.md` §1.**

### THE PART 6 THE WAR ROOM MADE

> **A null-temporality mapping accepts `current` and `unspecified` facts and
> refuses explicitly `historical`/`resolved` ones — a resolved finding must not
> fill a present-tense clause.**

⚠️ **THE SESSION'S MEASUREMENT WAS RIGHT AND ITS DIAGNOSIS WAS ONE LAYER TOO
SHALLOW.** It measured, correctly and with a control in both trees, that a
`temporality: null` `hpi.*` mapping stopped matching its own certification
self-test — `false` on the branch, `true` after the revert. It reported the
mechanism accurately: `verification-encounter.ts:356` dictates *"History of
present illness, …"* and `encounter-facts:239`'s `HISTORICAL` regex matches
`history of`.

**It then treated that regex as context rather than as the defect.**

> **"History of present illness" is a SECTION HEADER, not a temporal marker.**
> `hpi.*` facts are present-illness BY DEFINITION and must never be stamped
> `historical` by that phrase.

So part 6 did not break certification. **The projector broke certification, and
part 6 was the change that made the breakage visible.** A tightening that
exposes an existing defect is not the cause of it.

### THE RULED SEQUENCE — TWO CHANGES, IN THIS ORDER

**(a) #985 MERGES WITH `null ⇒ any`.** The revert stands **for this merge**: it
is `main`'s proven meaning, **0 of 185 production certified mappings can reach
the difference**, and it therefore loses nothing anyone holds. The session's
restraint here was correct even though its stated reason — an authority that
could not be produced — is now superseded by this ruling.

**(b) THE TIGHTENING LANDS AS ITS OWN CHANGE, AFTER #985, IN THIS ORDER INSIDE
ITSELF:**

1. **Fix the projector's `HISTORICAL` inference** to exclude the HPI header and
   `hpi.*` concepts. **Arm: the self-test's own HPI dictation yields `current`
   facts.**
2. **Only then** move the one constant in `fact-mapping-match.ts` to
   `null ⇒ current + unspecified`.
3. **Retire `gate-165`'s hardcoded `temporality: 'current'`** in the same
   change — it is the false-green that let (b)'s absence go unnoticed, and
   leaving it would let the next one do the same.
4. Its own adversarial pass, per the standing gate.

⚠️ **THE ORDER IS THE RULING, NOT AN IMPLEMENTATION NOTE.** Moving the constant
first re-breaks every HPI mapping's self-test, which is exactly the state #985
was reverted out of.

## #208 — OWNER-RULING — `conceptScope` IS A MAPPING, NOT A RENDER POLICY

**STANDING. Resolves the collision this session reported between #192/#193 and
#179(3) by denying that there is one.**

⚠️ **THE SESSION FRAMED IT AS TWO RULINGS PULLING OPPOSITE WAYS AND ESCALATED IT
AS UNDECIDABLE BELOW THE OWNER. THE FRAMING WAS WRONG, AND THE DISTINCTION IT
MISSED IS THE WHOLE ANSWER:**

| | what it is | who may author it |
|---|---|---|
| `unmentionedPolicy` (`keep`/`omit`) | a **RENDER POLICY** — what the machine does when nothing was dictated | **never the model** (#192, #193) |
| `clinicalConcept` | a **MAPPING** — which concept this field means | model PROPOSES, human CONFIRMS |
| **`conceptScope`** | **a MAPPING** — which concepts this clause covers | **model PROPOSES, human CONFIRMS** |

**So the model may propose `conceptScope` exactly as it proposes
`clinicalConcept`:**

- **VALIDATED against the closed lexicon — unknown tokens REFUSED AT PARSE**, not
  silently dropped and not silently kept;
- **SURFACED ON THE CONFIRM LIST**, where the doctor can see what a clause is
  claimed to cover;
- **CONFIRMED** by the doctor, or by auto-confirm under the *same* confidence
  rule that governs `clinicalConcept`;
- **never silently accepted.**

> **THE FIX IS VALIDATION + A CONFIRMATION SURFACE. IT IS NOT REMOVAL.**

⚠️ **AND THIS IS THE LAST CLUSTER GATING #985.** The PR stays DRAFT until
`conceptScope` is validated and confirmable; it merges after.

## #209 — OWNER-RULING — THE RECONSTRUCTION *IS* THE REGENERATION. NO RE-UPLOAD.

**STANDING. Answers the session's offer to have the owner re-upload his form,
and declines it — on the grounds that the reconstruction is not an approximation.**

The session rebuilt the 53-control form and offered it as *"a reconstruction,
not a recovery"*, saying only a re-upload could settle the clause boundaries.
**That caution was misplaced, and the reason is that both sides of the identity
are fixed:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **the same source bytes** | sha `6f04f29e…`, present on the box at `r5-handoff/GI Consultation Note - fillable.docx` |
| **the same deterministic rule** | the six sentences of paragraph 11; the ten exam value runs; the consent line; the time blank |

A regeneration is a rule applied to bytes. **Both are pinned, so the output is
the form — not a guess at it.**

**WHAT IS OWED INSTEAD OF A RE-UPLOAD:**

1. **Re-run the FIVE ADDITIVE PROOFS** on the regenerated form (#191: *"53
   controls as regenerated, all five additive proofs held"*).
2. **Record the derivation AS ITS PROVENANCE** — the rule, the source sha, and
   the arithmetic that pins which paragraphs are excluded.

**It is the form that certifies.**
