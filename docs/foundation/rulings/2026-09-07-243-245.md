## #244 — OWNER-RULING — THREE CANON ENTRIES

**STANDING. Bodies at `docs/canon/128`, `129`, `130`; rows in the INDEX.**

**128 · ASSERT A SQL MUTATION IN THE DATABASE, NOT IN THE FILE.** The file is a
source, not the state. `CREATE OR REPLACE FUNCTION` is idempotent and
**`CREATE TRIGGER` is not** — there is no `IF NOT EXISTS` — so a re-install
aborts part way and the database keeps the old build while the file shows the
new one. **A green control over an unapplied mutation is indistinguishable from
one over a guard that works.**

**129 · A WITNESS THAT ROLLS BACK WITH THE REFUSED TRANSACTION CANNOT WITNESS
THE REFUSAL.** The witness must survive the outcome it witnesses. **When a
witness reports a clean zero on exactly the arm where the guard fires, suspect
the witness before the guard.**

**130 · POSTGRES HAS NO NESTED TRANSACTIONS — A FIXTURE'S `COMMIT` ENDS THE
OUTER ONE.** The only signal is a WARNING on stderr among hundreds of lines.
`SAVEPOINT` and `SET LOCAL`; re-arm after a disarm; **assert the cleanup rather
than trusting the rollback.**

⚠️ **ALL THREE ARE INSTRUMENT FAILURES, NOT PRODUCT FAILURES**, and that is the
pattern worth naming. Every one of them produced a **GREEN** that meant nothing:
a control over code that was never installed, a cell whose witness could not
speak, a cleanup that had already committed. **This programme's remaining risk
is concentrated in what it uses to measure, not in what it measures.**
