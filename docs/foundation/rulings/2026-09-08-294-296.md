## #294 — OWNER-RULING — THE TENANT IS BILLED

**STANDING. CLASS (a) AND CLASS (d) TOGETHER — it is a statement about what a
visit IS, and it decides who is charged.**

> **A locum visit at practice A is A's chart, A's document, and A's usage.**

`worker.ts` binds `practiceId` from `visit.user.practiceId` — the AUTHOR's
practice — at a single line that feeds roughly twenty-five sites, admission and
charging among them. That binding is re-derived **from the VISIT row**, which is
the tenant, and it stays **ONE derivation** for all of them. Never from
`visit.user`.

⚠️ **AND THE TWENTY-FIVE ARE AUDITED BEFORE THE LINE MOVES, NOT AFTER.** A
single rename with twenty-five consumers is exactly the shape that half-applies
invisibly: some of those sites genuinely mean the AUTHOR.

| what the site means | what it takes |
|---|---|
| a doctor's own preferences, attribution, an audit row's actor | **`authorUserId`, explicitly** |
| scope, template selection, admission, charging | **the tenant** |

A site that means the author keeps the author **by name**, so the next reader
can see it was decided rather than missed.

**§2.5 ROW, DECLARED BEFORE THE ACT:** production holds **58/58 visits whose
author practice equals the tenant**, so this change moves **nothing** today. A
difference from that is THE FINDING, whatever explanation is available
afterwards.

**ARM:** on the three-identity fixture, **admission and charge hit A** — not the
locum's own practice B.
