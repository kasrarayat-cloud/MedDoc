## #302 — OWNER-RULING — THE SELECTION HALF IS #287's

**STANDING. It completes the ruling rather than extending it.**

> **The worker selects the TENANT practice's active template — the CHART's
> format — never the author's. `doctorId` is ATTRIBUTION ONLY.**

#287 said the tenant scopes the visit *and selects/validates the template*. Only
the VALIDATION half was applied: `fill-engine.ts` rejects a template the tenant
does not own, while `worker.ts` still SELECTS by
`resolveActiveTemplate({ doctorId: visit.userId })` and by a `picked` lookup keyed
`OR: [{ doctorId }, { isSystemDefault }]`. Two ends of one decision, on two axes.

⚠️ **AND THE HALF-APPLICATION IS WORSE THAN NEITHER HALF.** On `main` both ends
sat on the author and agreed: a locum's visit rendered the WRONG PRACTICE'S FORM
— wrong, and recoverable. Split, it yields **no document, a false explanation,
and (until the read-only pre-check) an irreversible pin.**

**APPLY IT AT `:771` AND `:777`.**

**THE ARM, on the three-identity fixture:**

1. a locum visit at practice **A** renders **A's active template**;
2. a tenant with **no** active template yields the named **`no_tenant_template`**
   miss — **never a pin, never a mismatch.**

## #303 — OWNER-RULING — THE STATUS DIMENSION IS DERIVED, AND THE ROWS ARE ADJUDICATED

**STANDING.**

Lens 4 measured `gate-review-defaults-omit-door`'s `statuses` and `detections` as
hand-written **2-of-4** literals while the schema accepts four — and the omitted
value is the column's **own default**, `NEEDS_CONFIRMATION`.

> **DERIVE THE DIMENSION FROM THE ENUM — #259's rule: enumerate from the
> source's own table, never from a hand-written list.**

⚠️ **AND THEN THE ROWS ARE ADJUDICATED, ONE AT A TIME IN KIND.** Deriving it
surfaces **1,344 disagreements** and **16,800 token mismatches**. Each is exactly
one of:

| verdict | meaning |
|---|---|
| **a DEFECT** | the offer and the honouring genuinely disagree — fix the code |
| **a LEGITIMATE DIVERGENCE** | correct, and the REASON is stated where the next reader meets it |
| **an INSTRUMENT ARTIFACT** | the matrix asks a question the product does not pose — fix the matrix |

> ⚠️ **NO SILENT ALLOWLIST.** A row suppressed without a verdict is the mirror
> this whole arc exists to end, wearing the gate's own clothes.

⚠️ **THE DANGEROUS DIRECTION IS ALREADY CLOSED and that is recorded so the work
is not mistaken for an emergency:** **zero** `offered && !honoured` rows across
all **3,483,648** legal combinations.

## #304 — OWNER-RULING — THE EXIT RULE

**STANDING. Same rule the twin primitive ran under.**

> **Lane 1's NEXT round is its LAST.**

| the round finds | what happens |
|---|---|
| **instrument-only** | repair the instruments → **ready** |
| **SUBJECT defects whose fix DELETES or NARROWS a path** | fix → **ready**, no further round |
| **a fix that ADDS a path** | that, and only that, earns another round |

**Lane 2 (#1036) runs under the identical rule.**

⚠️ The distinction is the whole clause: a deletion or a narrowing cannot
introduce a behaviour a lens has not seen, so re-rounding it buys nothing. A new
path can, and is the one case worth paying for again.
