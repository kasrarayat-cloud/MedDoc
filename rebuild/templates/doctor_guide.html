Your walkthrough

  1. Upload an unchanged blank Word form with AI preparation selected. Measure upload until both previews are visible; keep machine preparation and your active review time separate.
  2. Compare every question, choice, unit, instruction and answer location. Inspect both roles’ results and any uncertainty. The original remains unchanged.
  3. Record automatic errors before corrections. Save corrections with a reason, then explicitly approve each layout change and both documents. This creates an immutable version; later changes need a separately reviewed version.
  4. Use the approved version for a fictional document. Reuse it for a second document and confirm that preparation does not run again.
  5. Type exact labelled notes, save and generate once. Review each answer against its source. Missing information stays blank; conflicting or wrong-unit answers need your explicit resolution.
  6. For voice, use your actual browser microphone, then listen before submitting. Preserve the returned transcript, correct labels, numerals and units, and explicitly choose append or replace before saving it as notes.
  7. Generate from the reviewed transcript. Resolve held answers, review every answer and signing statement, finalize and download Word. Check the finalized document and its immutable mapping record.

Fictional typed SOAP case

For fictional patient Jordan Example, choose an approved SOAP form in your own authorized department. This example uses Subjective, Objective, Assessment and Plan. If your form has different labels, use its exact labels and keep the documented facts unchanged.

Subjective: Fictional follow-up: back pain was 6/10 last week and is 3/10 today. No new numbness is reported.
Objective: Synthetic clinic measurement: blood pressure 122/78 mmHg; heart rate 72/min.
Assessment: Fictional improving mechanical low-back pain.
Plan: Continue the documented home exercises; synthetic follow-up in two weeks.

Review every section against its documented source before finalization. Omit undocumented sections and explicitly review any blank answers. Do not infer missing clinical information.

Fictional microphone and unit check
