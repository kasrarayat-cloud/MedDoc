Supported forms

  • Word (.docx) with two-column question/answer tables, labelled underlined blanks (Question: ____), or {{Question}} placeholders.
  • Fillable PDF with visible text fields. Answers use plain printable characters.
  • The qualified WM IOV form, one specific Word file with dropdowns, recognised by its fingerprint. Upload it exactly as you received it; a copy opened and re-saved in Word is no longer recognised.

Refused at upload

Any other Word file with dropdowns, date pickers or other content controls; text boxes, tracked changes, fields or macros; scanned or flat PDFs; password-protected or encrypted files. The refusal names the reason.

Keep the form you know

Your original file is retained. Field mapping changes create a new version so documents keep the mapping they were generated with.
