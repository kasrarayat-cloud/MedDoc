Supported forms

  • Word (.docx) with two-column question/answer tables, {% templatetag openvariable %}Question{% templatetag closevariable %} placeholders, or lines made of a label and an underlined Tab: an ordinary paragraph (not inside a table), with nothing typed after the Tab. Typed underscores ____ are not answer fields.
  • Fillable PDF in which every field is a visible plain text field with a fixed font size (not Auto) in the standard Helvetica font, without links, attachments, format scripts or other actions, rotation, or read-only, comb or rich-text options. Answers use plain ASCII characters only (no curly quotes or apostrophes, long dashes, °, µ or accented letters) and must fit their box.
  • The qualified WM IOV form, one specific Word file with dropdowns, recognised by its fingerprint. Upload it exactly as you received it; a copy opened and re-saved in Word is no longer recognised.

Refused at upload

Any other Word file with dropdowns, date pickers or other content controls; text boxes, tracked changes, fields or macros; Word files with web or email links or linked images, including in a letterhead, header or footer; PDFs with checkboxes, radio buttons, dropdown, signature or button fields, or with links, attachments or actions; scanned or flat PDFs; password-protected or encrypted files and old .doc files. The refusal names the reason.

Keep the form you know

Your original file is retained. Field mapping changes create a new version so documents keep the mapping they were generated with.
