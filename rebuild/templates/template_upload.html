Check the supported layout first

  • Word (.docx): two-column question/answer tables, {% templatetag openvariable %}Question{% templatetag closevariable %} placeholders, or a label and an underlined Tab in an ordinary paragraph, not inside a table, with nothing typed after it. {% if form.prepare_with_ai %}With AI preparation selected, supported labelled underscores ____ can become answer anchors after you compare the original and candidate and explicitly approve the layout. Direct inspection does not treat underscores as fields.{% else %}Typed underscores ____ are not fields.{% endif %}
  • Fillable PDF: visible plain text fields with a fixed font size (not Auto) and standard Helvetica font. No rotation, read-only, comb or rich-text options. Answers must fit and use plain ASCII; curly punctuation, °, µ and accented letters are unsupported.
  • Qualified WM IOV: the qualified WM IOV form, one specific Word original with dropdowns, recognised by fingerprint. Upload it exactly as you received it; opening and re-saving it in Word changes that fingerprint.

Refused at upload

Word: tracked changes, fields, macros, web or email links or linked images, including headers and footers. {% if form.prepare_with_ai %}Other content controls and text boxes require assisted setup; AI preparation cannot approve unresolved regions. Direct inspection refuses these layouts.{% else %}Other content controls (including dropdowns and date pickers) and text boxes are also refused.{% endif %}

PDF: scanned or flat files; checkbox, radio, dropdown, signature or button fields; format scripts or other options. PDFs with links, attachments or actions, and PDFs that cannot be read completely, are refused.

Password-protected or encrypted files and old .doc files are also refused. This is not arbitrary Word compatibility.
