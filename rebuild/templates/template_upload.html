Check the supported layout first

  • Word (.docx): two-column question/answer tables, {% templatetag openvariable %}Question{% templatetag closevariable %} placeholders, or a label followed by an underlined Tab in an ordinary paragraph, outside a table, with nothing typed after it. Typed underscores ____ are not fields.
  • Fillable PDF: visible plain text fields using fixed-size Helvetica. No Auto font size, rotation, read-only, comb or rich-text options. Answers must fit and use plain ASCII; curly punctuation, °, µ and accented letters are unsupported.
  • Qualified WM IOV: one specific Word original with dropdowns, recognised by fingerprint. Upload the unchanged file; opening and re-saving it in Word changes that fingerprint.

Other layouts are refused

Word: other content controls (including dropdowns and date pickers), text boxes, tracked changes, fields, macros, web/email links or linked images, including headers and footers.

PDF: scanned or flat files; checkbox, radio, dropdown, signature or button fields; links, attachments, format scripts or other actions.

Password-protected or encrypted files and old .doc files are also refused. This is not arbitrary Word compatibility.
