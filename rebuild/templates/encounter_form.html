Labelled notes are required here

Question/answer tables, placeholders, fillable PDFs and the WM IOV form need labelled answers. An unlabelled line or an empty label sends every generated answer back for your decision. WM IOV also needs labelled prewritten statements; its form page lists them.

Missing information stays missing. Conflicting statements need your decision; they never become normal findings.

More limits: headings, corrections and underlined-Tab sentences can hold answers for review
  • A line ending in a colon is a heading. A sentence ending on following, below, as follows or the following plus one word also holds later answers for your decision.
  • A final heading (except a bare label such as Signed by:), or a heading with above, these, those or listed, holds earlier answers too. A correction holds earlier answers.
  • On WM IOV, a labelled answer ending in a colon, including a stray colon, holds that answer and later answers. Other labelled answers do not govern neighboring answers.
  • Underlined-Tab forms allow complete sentences with a recognised verb, such as is, was, has, had, reports or showed, and a final . ! or ?. A heading, quotation, unpunctuated extra line, or words such as if, these or above send answers back as conflicts.
