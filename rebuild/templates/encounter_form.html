Include the findings you actually documented. Missing information remains visible for review, and conflicting statements need your judgment.

Writing notes

Most reliable on every form: one Question label: answer per line, using each question's label exactly as the form page lists it. On forms with question/answer tables, placeholders, fillable PDFs and the WM IOV form, any line that is not a label with its answer (a label left empty counts) sends every generated answer back to you as a conflict to decide. A line ending in a colon is a heading, and so is a sentence that ends on following, below, as follows or the following and one word: every answer written after it needs your decision. A heading at the very end, unless it is a bare label such as Signed by:, or one with above, these, those or listed, also governs the answers before it. A correction makes the answers above it a decision. On the WM IOV form, labelled lines never govern. Write each denial as its own answer where you can (Symptoms: no chest pain). On underlined-Tab forms, sentences can be used, but each must be complete (with a verb QuillMD recognises, such as is, was, has, had, reports or showed, and ending in . ! or ?); a heading ending in a colon, a quotation, an unpunctuated extra line, or a word such as if, these or above sends the answers back as conflicts. The WM IOV form accepts only labelled lines, including one for each of its prewritten statements; its form page lists them.
