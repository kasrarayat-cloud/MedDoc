Writing notes

Most reliable on every form: one Question label: answer per line, using the form's own question wording. On every form, a heading line ending in a colon, a quotation, or a word such as if, would, these or above in the notes can send generated answers back to you as conflicts to decide; on forms with question/answer tables, any unlabelled line does. The qualified WM IOV form accepts only labelled lines, including one for each of its prewritten statements; its form page lists them.
