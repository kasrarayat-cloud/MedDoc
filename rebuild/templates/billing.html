Transparent usage

Know what each document costs.

Your usage charge is 1.5 × the attributable metered provider cost after applicable discounts and allocated provider credits, never more than the maximum shown before the request. Costs computed from configured rates are labelled as estimates until they are reconciled to authentic provider evidence; a reconciliation adjusts your ledger by exactly 1.5 × the difference, never above the limit you approved. Every request shows its maximum charge first, and that maximum is binding: anything above it is absorbed by QuillMD. Fixed platform overhead is tracked separately and never charged to you. The provider cost of a platform failure whose output you never received is absorbed by QuillMD, not charged to you. Personal promotional credit is tracked separately from paid funds.
